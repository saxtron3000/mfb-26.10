# Bitcoin Diploma design brief template

This neutral template intentionally contains no asset-specific source data. Populate it only from the current `workspace/08_assets.md` record when an asset is ready to be briefed.

## Source of truth

- Use `workspace/08_assets.md` for the approved asset definition, purpose, required format, description, placement, notes, and safety boundaries.
- Use `assets/style/STYLE.md`, `assets/style/CHARACTERS.md` when people appear, and `assets/style/layouts/README.md` for the applicable visual treatment.
- Use the relevant Student Guide and Educator Guide text to confirm placement headings and nearby markers.
- Record a completed brief location in `assets/assets-list.md` only after the brief has been reviewed and saved.

## Required content when populated

- Preserve the complete current asset-index row as a source snapshot.
- State the educational purpose, required format, detailed construction, elements, relationships, reading order, and qualifications.
- Record exact placement anchors for each applicable guide.
- Specify editable and translation-ready live text, meaningful alt text, source-editability needs, and colour, grayscale, print, and phone checks.
- Name the applicable reusable visual pattern, layout skeleton, palette roles, typography, geometry, connectors, icons, tables/data treatment, and character IDs, poses, and props where relevant.
- Record asset-specific safety restrictions, exclusions, production conditions, source conflicts, unresolved decisions, and completion criteria.

## Shared requirements

- Teach one clear idea with plain labels, familiar objects, an unambiguous reading order, and no decorative content.
- Keep final labels editable, short, and translatable; ensure the visual works in colour, grayscale, print, and on a phone.
- Use flat, print-safe MFB Angular Editorial Illustration: purple with white or paper surfaces, cool-gray dividers, controlled orange emphasis, clear negative space, and a 13° structural slant with upright content.
- Use existing graphic patterns and layout skeletons before proposing a reusable variant. Apply direct labels, colour-independent distinctions, and the relevant diagram or table construction rules.
- Use approved recurring characters only when people are needed; preserve their established identity and portray all learners as capable participants.
- Keep protocol, wallet, service, educator, user, node, and miner roles distinct whenever relevant. Do not depict Bitcoin in a wallet, miners making rules, or a single controller.
- Do not include financial, legal, tax, accounting, compliance, or investment advice; recommendations; guaranteed outcomes; real secrets, personal data, payment details, or live balances.
- Mark fictional interfaces, payment screens, and QR examples `SAMPLE — DO NOT PAY`. Do not imply a payable destination.
- Do not use crypto hype, generic blockchain imagery, gold coins, corporate-cartoon proportions, gradients, glossy or 3D effects, shadows, decorative clutter, unapproved icons, or redrawn official marks.

## Review and logging

Before marking a brief complete, verify that it faithfully reflects the current asset-index record and applicable style sources, includes all placement and safety requirements, and has meaningful alt text and production checks. Then record its exact path in the matching `Brief` cell of `assets/assets-list.md` and reconcile the design-brief total.
