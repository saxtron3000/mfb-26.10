**## BD-V-009**
**Type:** Illustration
**Purpose:** To illustrate three people who are unable to barter.
**Description:** This image shows three participants, Amina, Louis, and May, who all have three different objects. Amina has rice and wants shoes. Luis has shoes but wants cooking oil, not rice. May has cooking oil and wants repair work. This image shows three characters. In their position they have three objects which are represented as them holding, and then above them they have in a thought bubble three objects that they want. They're connected via arrows that show that they cannot trade what they need, what they have for what they want. The arrows show with red crosses that the barter is not possible. Use the character Bible to generate the three people.This illustration is three people, each with two objects, one they have and one they want, interconnected with arrows, showing the impossibility of barter. Each person has something that can't be traded.
