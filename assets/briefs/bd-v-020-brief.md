## Detailed visual specification

Helps learners compare why an asset may be chosen alongside its important trade-offs. The learner can explain one reason and one trade-off for each of three alternatives for holding value without receiving a personal investment recommendation.

### Composition and reading order

Pattern: Comparison table. Layout skeleton: `teaching-page`, within the guide figure region at the width stated above. Future reference orientation: landscape 16:9, 960 × 540. Preserve reading order and learner-readable type when adapting to the final print region; do not shrink a dense table to fit an arbitrary height.

Use a full-width three-column table: Alternative, Why people may choose it, Important trade-offs. Preserve three equally weighted rows: precious metals, real estate, stocks. Include restrained first-party object cues beside row names. Read across each alternative before moving to the next; keep possible benefit beside risk rather than splitting them into promotional and warning panels. Retain the current guide table until its approved replacement is ready.
