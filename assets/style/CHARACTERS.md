# My First Bitcoin Diploma — Character Bible

**Purpose:** lock the recurring cast so all people look as though they were drawn by the same in-house illustrator.

> Place the approved Character Bible artwork beside or inside this document. These written rules protect and deploy that approved artwork; they must not be used to redesign the cast from scratch.

## Shared visual lock

- Flat vector fills, restrained monoline outline and minimal interior detail.
- Realistic, slightly simplified adult/teen proportions.
- Minimal faces: simple eyes, optional nose and subtle calm mouth.
- Angular, simple clothing planes and stylised brand-tone skin/hair colours.
- The same face construction, hand construction, outline weight, clothing complexity and colour treatment in every pose.
- Friendly, capable and calm—not goofy, mascot-like or corporate-cartoon.

## Use rules

- Use character IDs in briefs and filenames, e.g. `C03_reading_v01`.
- Pair IDs with canonical names in briefs, e.g. `C03 Ernesto (older learner)`. Keep IDs and filenames stable when updating copy.
- Preserve approved visual details. Do not “improve” a character in one scene.
- Use Mei, the young learner, only in age-appropriate contexts.
- Ernesto, the older learner, is a capable active participant, never a stereotype or decorative inclusion.
- Do not make one role the expert in every scene; learners remain capable contributors.
- New poses, outfits, props or accessibility variants require review against this Bible before reuse.
