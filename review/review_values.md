# **I. Introduction to the Bitcoin Education Network**

## **Our Mission**

**Open source Bitcoin Education for the World.**

We have made our curriculum fully open source so that anyone, anywhere can teach Bitcoin. No permission from My First Bitcoin needed.

Whether you want to introduce Bitcoin education in a school, university, church or your local community, you are free to use, adapt, and translate our materials. Our goal is to make high-quality Bitcoin education as accessible as possible.

Using our open source curriculum does not require joining the [Bitcoin Education Network](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org). If you choose to build independently, we sincerely wish you success. There are many ways to contribute to Bitcoin education, and we believe a diversity of approaches strengthens the movement. But, learning from other peers is an important part of developing ourselves. That’s why we’re building a global network of educational communities: **the Bitcoin Education Network**.

## 

## **Building a Global Network for Bitcoin Education**

The Bitcoin Education Network exists to connect independent Communities that share a common commitment to high-quality Bitcoin education.

My First Bitcoin started this initiative, but every Community in the network is autonomous. Each Community is responsible for its own leadership, operations, fundraising, and local strategy. The network exists to support Communities, not to control them.

By sharing knowledge, best practices, and educational resources, we aim to accelerate Bitcoin education worldwide while preserving the independence of every Community.

## **Joining the Bitcoin Education Network**

Communities that align with the Bitcoin Education Network's principles may apply to join the **[Bitcoin Education Network](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org)**.

Joining the Leaderboard requires:

* Agreeing to uphold the 6 Community Principles by signing this Charter  
* Demonstrating active Bitcoin education in your local community.  
* Completing the onboarding process.

Once accepted, every Community is an equal member of the network.

Communities build reputation through the quality and consistency of their work. Reputation is publicly reflected on the My First Bitcoin Leaderboard and may unlock additional opportunities, support, or collaborations.

More information is available at:

[https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/)

# **II. Community Principles**

Every Community that joins the [Bitcoin Education Network](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org) agrees to uphold the following six principles.

1. ### **Independent**

   All education must remain independent of outside influence.

   Communities may accept sponsors, but sponsors must never influence classroom instruction, educational materials, or curriculum decisions. Acknowledging sponsors publicly is acceptable, but the education itself must remain free from commercial influence.

   We do not promote specific wallets, exchanges, financial products, or investment strategies. Our focus is understanding Bitcoin and the monetary system. Our focus is to teach HOW to think, not WHAT to think.

2. ### **Impartial**

   Bitcoin education should empower people to think for themselves.

   Educators should present Bitcoin and allow students to reach their own conclusions rather than promoting predetermined outcomes or personal opinions.

3. ### **Community-Based**

   Every Community understands its local context better than anyone else.

   Communities are encouraged to adapt their teaching methods, events, and outreach to best serve the people they educate while remaining aligned with the shared principles of the network.

   We teach bottom up, not top-down. We work on the ground and in classrooms, not to please governments and in office rooms.

   

4. ### **Bitcoin-Only**

   The Bitcoin Education Network focuses exclusively on Bitcoin education.

   Some financial literacy is naturally included because understanding Bitcoin requires understanding money. While local discussions may occasionally reference other technologies or financial systems, the curriculum remains centered on Bitcoin.

5. ### **Empowerment Over Profits**

   Bitcoin is a tool for individual freedom and long-term empowerment.

   We do not teach trading, price predictions, or investment strategies. Instead, we focus on helping people understand Bitcoin's role as an open monetary network and how it can increase individual sovereignty.

6. ### **Quality**

   We are committed to educational excellence.

   The community strives to deliver accurate, engaging, and impactful Bitcoin education. New Communities receive onboarding and guidance from experienced members of the network to help establish a strong foundation and maintain high educational standards.

## **III. Benefits of Joining the Bitcoin Education Network**

Joining the [Bitcoin Education Network](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org) connects your Community with a global network of Bitcoin educators who share knowledge, collaborate on initiatives, and support one another in advancing Bitcoin education.

Communities on the **My First Bitcoin Leaderboard** may unlock additional resources and opportunities as they build reputation through consistent, high-quality work. Examples of these perks include:

* A public Community profile and directory listing.  
* Personalized onboarding and ongoing support from the My First Bitcoin team.  
* Opportunities to be featured across My First Bitcoin's website, social media, newsletter, and podcast.  
* Access to fundraising resources, operational guides, and community-building tools.  
* Invitations to collaborate on network initiatives, events, and conferences.  
* Eligibility for additional educational, technical, and community support as new programs become available.  
* Ability to join the My First Bitcoin Board of Advisors (BoA), alongside other top communities as well as thought leaders in the space. See the current BoA at [https://myfirstbitcoin.org/about](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/about) 

The available perks, eligibility criteria, and Leaderboard structure may evolve over time as the network grows. For the most up-to-date information about the Leaderboard, reputation system, and available perks, visit: [**https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/**](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/)

## **IV. Sign the Charter**

This Charter establishes the Community identified below, and the Bitcoin Education Network constructed by My First Bitcoin.

My First Bitcoin is a nonprofit organization dedicated to creating and maintaining open source Bitcoin education. The Community independently uses these educational materials to teach Bitcoin within its local context.

### **My First Bitcoin commits to:**

* Maintain and improve the open source curriculum.  
* Share educational resources and best practices.  
* Provide onboarding guidance where appropriate.  
* Foster collaboration among Communities in the Bitcoin Education Network.\
* Maintain the My First Bitcoin Leaderboard and recognize Communities based on demonstrated work and reputation.

### **The Community commits to:**

* Uphold the six Community Principles outlined in this document.  
* Operate independently while remaining aligned with the mission of the Bitcoin Education Network.\
* Deliver honest, impartial, Bitcoin-only education.  
* Represent its work truthfully.  
* Continue actively educating people within its community.  
* Understand that participation on the Leaderboard, recognition, and additional opportunities are determined by My First Bitcoin based on demonstrated contribution, reputation, and alignment with the Community Principles.

This Charter remains in effect until either party chooses to end its participation. My First Bitcoin may also end this Charter if the Community no longer upholds the Community Principles outlined in this Charter or if continued collaboration is no longer appropriate.

This Charter reflects the full understanding between the parties regarding this relationship and replaces any previous agreements or understandings relating to it. Any changes to this Charter must be made in writing and agreed to by both parties.
