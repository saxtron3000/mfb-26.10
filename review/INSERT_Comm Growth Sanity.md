# [**1\. What is My First Bitcoin?**](https://network.myfirstbitcoin.io/t/1-start-here-what-is-my-first-bitcoin/16)  {#1.-what-is-my-first-bitcoin?}

My First Bitcoin is the leading Bitcoin Education non-profit in the world. We believe that Bitcoin Education will change the world, but not just any kind of education. We stand for Freedom Education.

We are building a global movement of **Open Source Bitcoin Education**.

A movement that is independent, impartial, and community-led. With education that empowers individuals, businesses, and communities with the knowledge and tools to understand money, gain financial literacy, and learn the deeper lessons that Bitcoin teaches us: critical thinking, personal responsibility, and ultimately sovereignty.

Our story began in 2021 as a grassroots movement in El Salvador, where we started teaching about Bitcoin in classrooms and communities. We quickly realized that accessible and impartial education could have a transformative impact.

**That’s why we created a complete educational curriculum, free and open for anyone, anywhere, so others could replicate and adapt it to their local context.** Everything we build reflects the future we hope to help create: a world where knowledge is open. Where people have agency in their life. And such empowerment begins with education.

Believing that education is the first step toward empowerment, we have built a complete ecosystem of programs and tools designed to equip educators with the knowledge, structure, and confidence to teach Bitcoin effectively, beginning within their own communities.

You’ll find more about our Resources in the third Wiki called Education Toolbox.

We approach our Education through a [Student Journey](https://journey.myfirstbitcoin.org/):

- A beginner shows interest in the courses and can join an **Introduction to Bitcoin** class. For youngsters we also have the **Bitcoin for Juniors** program.  
- A beginner becomes a real students when going through the Bitcoin Diploma  
- After finishing the Diploma, we offer the **Educator Training Program (ETP)** so you can become a more confident educator.  
- Once you graduate from the ETP, you are a certified educator\! Go into your community and become a community leader\! Now you can Join the **Education Network** with your Community and meet likeminded educators worldwide.

Today, My First Bitcoin is a global educational initiative present in more than 40 countries, dedicated to creating and sharing resources that anyone can use to teach Bitcoin and empower their peers. All our materials are free, adaptable under the CC 4.0 license, and open source, those principles that will always remain at the heart of everything we do.

Our goal is simple: to make impartial Bitcoin education available to everyone, everywhere, without compromise or external influence. We refuse sponsorships that could affect our independence and are proudly supported by the Bitcoin community itself, proof that collective action can build something powerful and lasting.

If you believe education is the foundation of freedom, you’re already part of this mission. Learn more about My First Bitcoin and explore our open resources [here](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/learn/programs).

# [**2\. What is the Education Network?**](https://network.myfirstbitcoin.io/t/2-what-is-the-my-first-bitcoin-education-network/30)  {#2.-what-is-the-education-network?}

The **My First Bitcoin Education Network** is a global alliance of independent Bitcoin educators and grassroots projects working together to bring independent and Open Source Bitcoin Education to the World.

It began with a simple idea, that education can spark real change.

My First Bitcoin started teaching in 2021, and within just a few years, that spark grew into a worldwide movement. Today, dozens of projects across more than 40 countries are using our free and open source materials to teach Bitcoin in their own languages, online and in person, from classrooms to community meetups.

Each project in the network operates independently but stays connected through shared values:

* Independence with zero external influence.  
* Impartiality.  
* A community-based approach.  
* Bitcoin-only focus.  
* Empowerment over profit.  
* Commitment to quality.

The My First Bitcoin Education Network is self-governing, educators from every region meet regularly in **Monthly General Assemblies** to share proof of work, exchange ideas, and support one another. This collaboration strengthens local impact while building a truly global movement. We share best practices, exchange resources and ideas and share challenges. All with the same motto: give more away than you take away.

Everyone in the network is equal and independent. You can find a list of all of us in the [Leaderboard](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/), which also attempts to bring insights in each individual's achievements, milestones and contributions.

Together, these communities represent the next chapter of Bitcoin adoption: **working locally, connected globally**, and united by a shared belief that independent education will change the world.

# [**4\. Train Your Team and Yourself**](https://network.myfirstbitcoin.io/t/4-train-your-team-and-yourself/32)  {#4.-train-your-team-and-yourself}

Before you teach others, it’s essential to build your own confidence and understanding. Teaching Bitcoin isn’t just about knowledge, it’s about clarity, preparation, and connection. That’s why at My First Bitcoin, we’ve developed a complete path to help you and your team get ready to deliver Open Source Bitcoin Education effectively.

Start by reading the Bitcoin Diploma thoroughly, not once, but twice. Familiarize yourself with the flow of the chapters and the key concepts. Then move to the [Educators Guide](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/en/educator-guide/), which breaks down each class, offering structure, timing, and suggested activities. These two resources together form the foundation for every great Bitcoin class.

Next, explore our additional training tools. Download the [presentation slides](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/) to support your lessons. Observing others in action is one of the best ways to learn, and you can also ask for mentorship from the Community to receive guidance, feedback, and practical support as you prepare to teach. Email [arnold@myfirstbitcoin.org](mailto:arnold@myfirstbitcoin.org) if you need help\!

When you feel ready, you can take the next step by joining the [Educator Training Program](https://edu.myfirstbitcoin.io/course/educator-training-program), a structured course designed to refine your teaching skills and align you with the values and standards of the My First Bitcoin community. Upon completion, you’ll earn the Certified Educator badge, a recognition of your commitment to independent, high-quality Bitcoin education.

Every great educator starts as a learner. Take the time to train yourself and your team, your future students will thank you for it.

# [**5\. ‘Start Your First Cohort**](https://network.myfirstbitcoin.io/t/5-start-your-first-cohort/31)  {#5.-‘start-your-first-cohort}

Once you’ve trained and prepared your team, it’s time to take the next big step, starting your first cohort of the Bitcoin Diploma. This moment marks the beginning of your journey as an educator, and it’s where theory becomes a real impact.

The Bitcoin Diploma can be taught flexibly, based on the needs of your students. Begin by deciding where and how you’ll teach. A physical classroom offers stronger connections and a sense of community; a virtual one offers flexibility and reach. If you need something more robust, My First Bitcoin also offers access to our MFB App, ideal for tracking progress and hosting multiple courses. This is in closed beta, please reach out to [arnold@myfirstbitcoin.org](mailto:arnold@myfirstbitcoin.org) if you want to get early access.

Once your environment is ready, enroll your students. Share your class invites on social media or within your local community, even small groups are a great start. Tag My First Bitcoin, so we can amplify your work\! Use the [slides](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/) and apply everything you learned in the [ETP](https://etp.myfirstbitcoin.org/).

Teaching your first class may feel like a challenge, but it’s also one of the most rewarding experiences you’ll have. Every student you teach becomes part of a growing movement toward freedom and self-sovereignty through education.

When you’re ready to begin, everything you need to organize and launch your first cohort is available.

Finally, make sure you benefit from the knowledge in the Education Network. If you’re looking for a co-tutor, please request help from arnold@myfirstbitcoin.org.

# [**6\. Finish Your First Cohor**](https://network.myfirstbitcoin.io/t/6-finish-your-first-cohort/34)**t** {#6.-finish-your-first-cohort}

Reaching the end of your first Bitcoin Diploma cohort is a milestone worth celebrating. You and your students have completed a journey of learning, discovery, and empowerment, and that deserves recognition.

A graduation ceremony is more than just a formality; it’s a celebration of growth, both personal and collective. It reinforces the sense of community and reminds everyone involved that education is the foundation of freedom. You can host the ceremony wherever it feels meaningful, in your classroom, at a local café, or any space that reflects the spirit of your project.

Prepare certificates for your students as a tangible symbol of their achievement. You can easily customize [this template in Canva](https://canva.link/55s417wnb9ehdwo). 

Reviewing these resources carefully will help streamline the process and ensure consistency across all issued certificates.

Graduations are also powerful opportunities to share your story. Capture photos or short videos, invite local media or community members, and post your experience online using the hashtag **\#MyFirstBitcoinGraduation**. Celebrating your students publicly inspires others to join, learn, and even start their own classes. That’s how grassroots education grows.

Whether big or small, your first graduation marks the proof of your Open Source Bitcoin Education in action, knowledge shared freely, changing lives one class at a time. After finishing new cohorts, the next step is to send the amount of graduates to arnold@myfirstbitcoin.org so it can be included in our [Bitcoin Education Leaderboard](https://leaderboard.myfirstbitcoin.org/).

You can use the same email to request Arnold’s participation as a guest speaker on behalf of My First Bitcoin for online graduations or X Spaces, helping celebrate milestones and connect local efforts with the broader network\!

**8\. Set up your Profile in Leaderboard**

The Education Network Leaderboard is a public record of the local communities teaching Bitcoin in the My First Bitcoin Education Network, and of the work each of them has done. Joining is free. It takes about an hour, and most of your first points come from things you already have: a name, a logo, a website and your social accounts.

The My First Bitcoin team reads your email with the signed Charter, sets up your community profile and adds you to the leaderboard. In the future this will go fully automated, but we’re still testing our systems.

Your community then has its own page, and it appears in the list of communities on the reporting form. You cannot report points before this step: the reporting form only lists communities that are already on the leaderboard.

Every rule and its point value is public on the Transparency page. The values below are copied from it on 2026-09-28; the Transparency page is always the up-to-date version.

- Community name  
- Country / location  
- Team members (names and roles)  
- Languages you teach in  
- Website and social accounts  
- Relevant links (past events, media coverage, anything else worth a look)  
- What you are teaching and where you are headed next

Tip: this email is where your first points come from. Everything in Part 2's "profile" list is scored from what the team records from this email, so include all of it now (logo file, Lightning address, fundraising link, every social account) rather than sending it bit by bit later.

Profile points: one email, well over 100 points  
These are one-time points for having a complete profile. They count in every time window on the leaderboard (marked "Always" on the Transparency page), so they never expire.

# [**3\. Learning Materials: Print Your Books**](https://network.myfirstbitcoin.io/t/3-learning-materials-educational-resources/44)  {#3.-learning-materials:-print-your-books}

High-quality learning materials are essential for effective Bitcoin education. As part of the Education Network, you have full access to open-source educational resources that you can adapt and use in your local context.

This kit helps Communuties choose the best strategy for printing the open-source Bitcoin Diploma workbook in their own language. Each option has pros and cons, so choose what best fits your situation.

**Print on Demand (Any Quantity)**  
The English and Spanish workbooks are available via:

* [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/shop/my-first-bitcoin/bitcoin-diploma-2025/paperback/product-q6g2k46.html?q=Bitcoindiploma&page=1&pageSize=4)  
* [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FMQ3YRRR?ref=cm_sw_r_ffobk_cp_ud_dp_7DSWP1BS3JQRE6CRYVNA&ref_=cm_sw_r_ffobk_cp_ud_dp_7DSWP1BS3JQRE6CRYVNA&social_share=cm_sw_r_ffobk_cp_ud_dp_7DSWP1BS3JQRE6CRYVNA&bestFormat=true)  
* [Konsensus](https://bitcoinbook.shop/products/bitcoin-diploma?Format=Paperback)

This option allows you to print any number of copies, even a single book.

**Local Print Shop (Any Quantity)**  
You can [download](https://myfirstbitcoin.org/bitcoin-diploma-course/) the workbook PDF and print it locally. This gives you full control, but keep in mind:

* Formatting and colors may not be optimized  
* Print quality can vary  
* Costs may be higher for small runs

**Network Print Shops (Any Quantity)**

If you don’t have access to a reliable local printer, we may be able to help. We work with a network of high-quality print shops in several countries. To explore this option, contact arnold@myfirstbitcoin.org.

**Bitcoin Publishing House (50+ Copies)**

For print runs of 50 books or more, you may work with Konsensus Network, a Bitcoin-native publishing house. Important notes:

* Funding must be secured in advance  
* Books are printed in Europe, so shipping costs may apply  
* Larger orders reduce per-unit cost

Example estimates:

* \~300 books: \~\$6.50 per book  
* \~500 books: \~\$5.40 per book  
* Smaller runs may cost more

**Print together with Other Communties**

If other nodes in your country are printing the same workbook, consider placing a shared order to reduce costs. Nodes are encouraged to share printing experiences and costs in the comments to help others make informed decisions.

**Network Educational Resources**

In addition to the workbook, many nodes have created additional educational resources based on My First Bitcoin materials. These are stored in the Education Network Resources [folder on GitHub](https://github.com/MyFirstBitcoin). You are free to:

* Download and use these materials  
* Adapt them for your community  
* Share your own resources

When possible, please credit the original creator.

**Build and Share Together**

All resources are open source and continuously evolving. By sharing your experiences and materials, you help strengthen the entire Education Network and make Bitcoin education more accessible worldwide.

# [**6\. Funding & Sustainability**](https://network.myfirstbitcoin.io/t/6-funding-sustainability/47)  {#6.-funding-&-sustainability}

As your project grows, you may need funding to sustain operations or responsibly expand your impact. Within the Education Network, funding is approached with care and intention, prioritizing Bitcoin-native, ethical, and transparent models aligned with independent education.

Funding is not the goal. Sustainability without capture is.

Independent Bitcoin education only works when funding reinforces the mission rather than compromising it. Funding choices should protect:

* Independence.  
* Impartiality.  
* Community trust.

Bitcoin teaches us to lower time preference and think long term. Applied to funding, this means:

* Building diverse sources of support.  
* Maintaining clear separation between funding and education.  
* Establishing firm boundaries.  
* Treating funding as a tool for sovereignty, not dependency.

Who funds your project matters, because over time funding relationships shape incentives and behavior.

**When to say no to funding**

Just as Bitcoin resists capture, educational projects must remain vigilant. Be cautious when funding comes with:

* Attempts to influence what or how you teach.  
* Pressure to promote products or narratives.  
* Restrictions on critical thinking.  
* Growth incentives that prioritize quantity over quality.

Declining misaligned funding is not a setback. It is an opportunity to protect your mission and lead by example. Before accepting any funding, take time to reflect:

* What are their interests?  
* Who else do they fund?  
* What do they expect in return?  
* How might this relationship evolve over time?  
* Does it strengthen or threaten your independence?

If the answers are unclear, it is worth pausing.

**Grassroots and Bitcoin-native funding**

For most early-stage projects, grassroots support and Bitcoin-native tools are the natural place to start. [Geyser](http://geyser.fund/) is a Bitcoin-native crowdfunding platform that allows projects to:

* Receive donations directly in Bitcoin.  
* Operate without a legal entity.  
* Accept Bitcoin and Lightning.  
* Launch campaigns quickly and transparently.

This makes Geyser well suited for early-stage projects covering operational costs while maintaining independence. To get started, follow the [Geyser guide](https://guide.geyser.fund/geyser-docs).

**Other Bitcoin-native options include:**

* Direct Bitcoin or Lightning addresses.  
* My First Bitcoin’s BTCPay Server for Education Network members.  
* Self-hosted BTCPay instances for maximum independence.

**Building a strong foundation**

Sustainable funding follows strong foundations. Projects with:

* A clear vision.  
* Defined roles.  
* Measurable objectives.  
* A realistic budget.

Build trust and reduce the risk of mission drift.

**Open Source Everything repository**

To support projects in building responsibly from the start, My First Bitcoin maintains the [Open Source Everything](https://github.com/MyFirstBitcoin/Open-Source-Everything) repository. This repository contains the operational tools, templates, and frameworks used by My First Bitcoin itself, shared openly with the Education Network. Please note this is still work in progress and some folders are still emptu.

While especially useful for Communities preparing to scale, these resources are a recommended starting point for any project that wants to establish strong foundations early. Communities can adapt these tools to structure:

* Operations.  
* Finances.  
* Communications.  
* Internal processes.

The repository includes resources related to:

* Education.  
* Communications.  
* Finance and administration.  
* Operations and fundraising.  
* Technology.

All materials are open source and designed to be adapted, improved, and reused. Using these tools helps projects build credibility, reduce operational risk, and prepare responsibly before pursuing larger funding opportunities.

**Transparency, growth, and sustainability**

Funding tends to follow proof of work. Consistently documenting activities, sharing impact, and engaging your local community builds credibility and attracts aligned support. Transparency is essential. A simple and sustainable practice is to publish regular updates that combine:

* A brief financial overview.  
* A short impact summary.

**Institutional and grant-based funding**

Institutional or grant-based funding may be considered if grassroots support is no longer sufficient. This step is optional and should only be pursued if it aligns with your values, capacity, and long-term vision. If grassroots tools like Geyser already cover your needs, institutional funding is not required.

Although many fundraising resources are written with the United States in mind, their principles and steps can often be adapted to other jurisdictions.

* My First Bitcoin does not fund Education Network projects directly. In some cases.  
* My First Bitcoin may connect or recommend projects to grant facilities when appropriate, but funding decisions always remain independent.

Most grant facilities require a 501(c)(3) status or equivalent legal structure. Grant funding should reinforce your mission, not reshape it. We explain how to set this up in the previously linked [Startup Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1SvygZ--fs123Y3oibEQOmr7mTBDMg0Ah44COfQ5ycHw/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.en51v8ybc3v0).

Some grant facilities aligned with My First Bitcoin’s work include:

* ***Paystand** – Supports nonprofits advancing Bitcoin-based circular economies in underserved communities.*  
* ***OpenSats** – Funds open-source Bitcoin projects focused on education, development, and infrastructure.*  
* ***Block** – Supports community-driven projects advancing economic empowerment and open-source infrastructure.*  
* ***Human Rights Foundation (HRF)** – Supports initiatives advancing human rights through Bitcoin education.*  
* ***Reynolds Foundation** – Supports education projects promoting freedom, democracy, and community impact.*  
* ***StartSmall** – Supports open-source projects focused on Girls’ Health & Education and Open Internet Development.*

**Build sustainably**

Funding should enable your work, not control it. Grow at a pace your community can sustain, protect trust, and remain grounded in independence. To further support your preparation, My First Bitcoin provides a [Fundraising Kit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Uqjk-DPFKg43mx9YUZiuN849rLP0aY6iTYbx3O_S1Gk/edit) with tools, processes, and policies for responsible fundraising.

# [**7\. Contribute & Grow**](https://network.myfirstbitcoin.io/t/7-contribute-grow/42)  {#7.-contribute-&-grow}

**Improve the Network Together**  
My First Bitcoin is a community-led project. Our educational materials and tools evolve through feedback from educators actively using them. You’re encouraged to:

* Share suggestions to improve the Bitcoin Diploma  
* Propose ideas to strengthen the My First Bitcoin Education Network and its tools

If you create additional materials for your local community, you can share them so other educators benefit as well. Ways to contribute:

* Upload resources to the [Education Network Resources](https://github.com/MyFirstBitcoin/Node-Network/tree/main/OS%20Everything) folder  
* Review existing materials on GitHub

These contributions help new projects start faster and strengthen Bitcoin education globally.

**Keep Building**

Your work as a Communinty matters. Every class taught, every student reached, and every resource shared contributes to a more informed and empowered global Bitcoin community. Thank you for being part of the My First Bitcoin Education Network, and for helping build the future of independent Bitcoin education.
