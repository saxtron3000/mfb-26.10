## 3. Brand Voice & Tone

### The MFB Voice

A passionate educator who deeply believes in what they're doing — direct, warm, mission-driven, and unapologetic about the importance of independence.

### Tone Attributes

| Attribute | We are | We are NOT |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Bold**  | Take strong positions with conviction | Aggressive, attacking, combative |
| **Empowering** | "Join the movement" | Preachy — "you should care about this" |
| **Warm**  | Real people, celebrate wins, human | Cold, corporate, institutional |
| **Clear** | No jargon, no assumed knowledge | Technical, exclusionary, elitist |
| **Mission-driven** | Everything ties back to why we exist | Self-promotional or transactional |

### Key Phrases (use these)

* "Education that Empowers"
* "Teach how to think, not what to think"
* "Power-to, not power-over"
* "Independent Bitcoin education"
* "Empowering those that empower others"
* "The revolution is inside our own minds"
* "Fiercely independent"
* "Open source everything"
* "Join the community"

### Channel-Specific Tone

| Channel | Tone adjustment |
|---------|-----------------|
| Twitter/X | Punchier, more direct, bold statements |
| Blog / Long-form | Deeper, more narrative, educational |
| Donor communications | Warmer, gratitude-forward, impact-focused |
| Educator materials | Clear, supportive, encouraging |
| Internal (Mattermost) | Casual, collaborative, human |
