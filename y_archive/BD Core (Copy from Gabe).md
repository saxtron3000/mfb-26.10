## Module 3 - Using and securing Bitcoin (Self-custody)

* Custodial vs non custodial (quick explanation)
  * Custodial: You have an account, an identifier within the app's database. The app allows you to view your bitcoin balance and send and receive Bitcoin. These are good for daily spending but not savings. You do not hold your own keys.
  * Non custodial: You hold your own keys, you have 12 or 24 words written down that give you control over your Bitcoin. The app or device allows you to view your bitcoin balance and send and receive Bitcoin. These are good for savings, and depending on the app, could also be used for daily spending. You hold your own keys.
* Hot vs cold wallets
  * Hot: Your seed phrase is generated and kept on a device that can connect to the internet. These apps allow you to create your seed phrase, view your balance, and send/receive Bitcoin. They're not "unsecure" but they have a larger attack vector than cold wallets. Nothing wrong with using them for savings, but your security could be better.
  * Cold: The gold standard of self-custody. These devices allow you to create your seed phrase and sign transactions while remaining completely offline. Securing your Bitcoin with a cold/hardware wallet is the best option for your Bitcoin. Today we're going to learn how to use them properly.
  * Two routes (pick one)
    * Hot wallet set up + send and receive using testnet or signet
    * Cold wallet set up + send and receive using testnet or signet
