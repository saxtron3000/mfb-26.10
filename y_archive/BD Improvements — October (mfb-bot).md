## ALIGNMENT & STRUCTURE ISSUES

### Issue 1: Missing Visual Asset Specification

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Teachers struggle to find and use referenced visuals

**Details:**

* The Teacher's Guide references charts, diagrams, and visuals throughout (e.g., "The visual on page 12," "chart on page 7") but doesn't specify:
  * Where these assets live (separate PDFs? Sanity? Outline?)
  * What each visual depicts
  * Whether all visuals are complete in the current Diploma

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Audit all referenced visuals in the Diploma
* Create a master Asset Map linking visuals to their location
* Ensure all charts/diagrams mentioned in the guide actually exist in the published version
* Consider creating alt-text and low-res versions for offline use


---

### Issue 2: Diploma-to-Guide Mapping Unclear

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Teachers don't know whether to teach from Diploma or Guide, or how they integrate

**Details:**

* The Teacher's Guide doesn't explicitly map to Diploma chapters/pages
* No clarity on which content is mandatory reading vs. teacher-delivered
* Unclear how activities in the Diploma relate to sections in this guide

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Create a cross-reference document: "Teaching from the Diploma + Guide"
* Specify for each chapter whether students should read Diploma text directly or teacher should paraphrase
* Link each guide section to its corresponding Diploma page(s)

## CONTENT GAPS (Diploma Level)

### Issue 3: Chapter 3–4 (Fiat/Problems) Lacks Localization

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Content assumes US context; may not resonate globally

**Details:**

* Examples of monetary policy, inflation, and government control are heavily US-focused (Federal Reserve, 1913, Roosevelt, Bretton Woods, 1971)
* Teacher's Guide notes "discuss in culturally sensitive ways" but Diploma content doesn't provide non-US examples
* Chapter 4 about "problems" uses mostly Western examples of inequality and debt

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Add 2–3 regional case studies (El Salvador, Zimbabwe, Venezuela, Argentina, Kenya, etc.)
* Include examples of how different governments have handled monetary crises
* Provide context about where the Diploma is being taught and what local monetary systems look like
* Develop a localization template teachers can use


---

### Issue 4: Chapter 6–7 (Wallets/Lightning) Assumes High Connectivity

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Guide is impractical in low-bandwidth or offline-heavy environments

**Details:**

* Chapter 6 and 7 demo live wallet setup, requiring:
  * Smartphone or computer per student (or group)
  * Reliable internet for wallet download and transactions
  * Ability to execute real transactions or testnet access
* No low-tech alternatives or offline-first approaches
* No guidance for teachers in areas with unreliable internet

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Develop a "Low-Connectivity" variant of Chapters 6–7
* Create paper wallet exercises or simulations for offline learning
* Document testnet wallets and how to set them up
* Provide slide decks/videos that can be pre-loaded and used offline
* Add contingency activities if technology fails mid-lesson


---

### Issue 5: Chapter 10 (Future/CBDCs) May Date Quickly

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Content about CBDCs and future developments will become outdated

**Details:**

* CBDC development is rapid; landscape changes quarterly
* Current examples (Canada bank freeze, etc.) may not be current by Oct 2026
* Speculative content about "future adoption" may feel dated

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Plan quarterly update schedule for Chapter 10 (or equivalent)
* Create a "Current Events" supplement teachers can add
* Build a framework for teaching about evolving technology without committing to specifics
* Consider whether Chapter 10 should be more reflective/philosophical vs. current-events-based

## PEDAGOGICAL GAPS (Diploma Level)

### Issue 6: Limited Scaffolding for Complex Concepts (Chapters 3, 8–9)

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Less experienced teachers and struggling students may not grasp technical concepts

**Details:**

* Chapter 3 (Fractional Reserve Banking) and Chapter 8–9 (Technical/Mining) are conceptually dense
* Limited step-by-step buildup or alternative explanations for different learning styles
* No "easier version" or "harder version" of content

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Develop animated or video explanations for complex concepts
* Create 2–3 simplified versions of Chapters 3, 8, 9 (for different audiences)
* Add more analogies and real-world examples
* Consider interactive simulations (e.g., fractional reserve banking game, proof-of-work simulator)


---

### Issue 7: Insufficient Diversity in Student Perspectives

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Content may not speak to all learner motivations or backgrounds

**Details:**

* While the Teacher's Guide mentions "5 student avatars," the Diploma content doesn't consistently speak to them
* Examples are primarily economically-focused; less for:
  * Students interested in technology/cryptography primarily
  * Students in strong economies (vs. weak monetary systems)
  * Students with trauma around money or financial systems
  * Students with no formal financial education

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Audit Diploma for inclusivity of different student motivations
* Add examples that speak to tech-first, privacy-first, and community-first learners
* Include narratives from people in different economic contexts
* Develop content warnings or facilitation notes for financially sensitive topics


---

### Issue 8: Assessment Criteria Undefined in Diploma

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Teachers can't assess whether students actually learned the material

**Details:**

* Learning objectives are clear, but no rubrics or evidence rubrics show what mastery looks like
* No suggested assessments embedded in Diploma chapters
* Guide mentions "check for understanding" but no systematic approach

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Create assessment rubrics for each chapter (what counts as "got it" vs. "still learning")
* Develop 1–2 formative assessment tools per chapter
* Suggest capstone projects or final assessments that integrate learning
* Create answer keys or evaluation guides for quizzes

## TECHNICAL/PRACTICAL GAPS (Diploma Level)

### Issue 9: Wallet Recommendations May Change

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Recommended wallets become outdated or unavailable

**Details:**

* Chapter 6–7 recommend specific wallets (implied by examples)
* Wallet landscape changes; recommended tools may be deprecated
* No guidance on how to choose alternative wallets with same properties

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Create a "Wallet Evaluation Checklist" so teachers can vet new wallets
* Develop a list of 3–5 backup wallet recommendations with feature comparison
* Document the process for updating wallet recommendations quarterly
* Ensure recommendation criteria are clear, not just "we like this one"


---

### Issue 10: Merchant/Adoption Examples May Not Be Current

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Examples of Bitcoin adoption become outdated or inaccurate

**Details:**

* Chapter 7 mentions BTCMap, BTCPay Server, Bitcoin Beach, Arnhem, etc.
* These examples need regular verification (do merchants still accept? Is the website live?)
* No "example currency" to compare Bitcoin's adoption to over time

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Create a monthly "adoption data snapshot" showing Bitcoin acceptance metrics
* Document which examples are "stable" (unlikely to change) vs. "living" (need updating)
* Build a process for regular verification of merchant and adoption examples

## TRAINING & TEACHER PREP GAPS

### Issue 11: No Explicit Connection to ETP (Educator Training Program)

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Trainers using ETP don't know how this guide fits into their training

**Details:**

* This guide is for teaching the Diploma, but ETP trainees also need guidance on:
  * How to teach educators to use this guide
  * How this guide scaffolds with ETP content
  * How to assess trainer competency on "can teach using this guide"

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Create a "Trainer's Guide to the Teacher's Guide"
* Map ETP learning outcomes to this guide's components
* Develop rubrics for assessing whether a trainer can facilitate effectively with this guide
* Create train-the-trainer modules


---

### Issue 12: Teacher Confidence Prerequisites Undefined

**Status:** Deferred to Oct\n**Impact:** Teachers may use guide without foundational Bitcoin knowledge

**Details:**

* This guide assumes teachers understand Bitcoin at a certain level
* No explicit guidance on: "Before you teach from this guide, you should be able to..."
* May set up unprepared teachers for failure

**Action for Oct 26.10:**

* Create a "Teacher Self-Assessment" checklist
* Develop a "Minimum Viable Knowledge" guide for educators
* Build a pre-teaching orientation video or module
* Link to ETP as the "official prep" for teachers new to Bitcoin education
