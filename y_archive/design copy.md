# Additional Design Principles

## Preserve Thematic Coherence

The redesigned Diploma should not become a collection of disconnected modules.

Thematic consistency should be intentionally preserved across:

- money
- economics
- technology
- Bitcoin
- sovereignty
- responsibility

The learner should experience a coherent narrative rather than a collection of lessons.

---

## Preserve Conceptual Progression

The Diploma should continue moving from foundational concepts toward more advanced concepts.

Design decisions should avoid:

- introducing complexity too early;
- assuming prerequisite knowledge;
- disrupting conceptual progression.

---

## Design For Conversations

The Diploma should not simply transfer information.

It should intentionally create:

- conversations;
- reflection;
- dialogue;
- perspective comparison;
- critical thinking.

Learning experiences should generate discussion rather than merely deliver content.

---

## Design For Independent Reasoning

The goal is not agreement.

The goal is independent thought.

Learners should leave with stronger reasoning capabilities rather than memorized conclusions.

---

# Additional Focus Area

## Educational Category Clarity

The current Diploma often moves between:

- historical explanation;
- economic explanation;
- technical explanation;
- philosophical interpretation;
- practical instruction.

The redesign should make these distinctions clearer.

Learners should be able to distinguish:

- facts;
- models;
- opinions;
- interpretations;
- practical guidance.

---

# Additional Focus Area

## Cognitive Load Management

The redesign should deliberately reduce unnecessary cognitive load.

Methods may include:

- reducing repetition;
- improving sequencing;
- reducing unnecessary technical depth;
- improving hierarchy of concepts;
- clarifying prerequisites.

The objective is not simplification.

The objective is improved learning efficiency.

---

# Additional Non-Negotiables

- Preserve the Diploma's recognizable identity.
- Preserve its community-learning orientation.
- Preserve dialogue as a central learning mechanism.
- Preserve conceptual coherence across the program.
- Preserve accessibility of language.
- Avoid corporate or institutional educational tone.
- Avoid technical complexity that does not support learner competence.

---

# Additional Learner Experience Goals

The learner experience should be:

- coherent;
- discussion-rich;
- intellectually serious;
- practical;
- confidence-building;
- globally adaptable;
- accessible to beginners.

---

# Additional Design Risks

## Fragmentation Risk

Modularization may improve flexibility while weakening narrative coherence.

The redesign should guard against this.

---

## Over-Optimization Risk

Reducing duration must not reduce:

- understanding;
- reflection;
- discussion;
- competence.

---

## Adaptation Risk

Localization and future AI adaptation should not weaken:

- standards;
- learning objectives;
- conceptual integrity;
- educational quality.

---

# Additional Design Questions

- What themes must appear throughout the entire Diploma?
- What concepts form the backbone of the learner journey?
- What conversations should learners still be having after completing the Diploma?
- What assumptions can safely be removed?
- What complexity genuinely contributes to learner competence?
- What complexity only increases cognitive load?
