# Bitcoin Diploma Review, SWOT Analysis & Redesign Scope of Work

## TL;DR

The Bitcoin Diploma has already established itself as a leader in grassroots Bitcoin education globally. This redesign is not about replacing the identity or foundations of the diploma. It is about maintaining relevance, improving effectiveness, and increasing long-term educational impact as Bitcoin education matures.

Research, educator feedback, learner feedback, and implementation experience have identified several structural weaknesses that now limit scalability and learner retention. The most significant issue is duration and attrition, alongside unclear pacing, vague scope, weak competency mapping, inconsistent review systems, excessive technical depth for beginners in some areas, and insufficient educator support systems. The redesign aims to create a shorter, clearer, more modular, more practical, and more captivating diploma without reducing educational quality or learner outcomes.

The redesign roadmap includes restructuring the diploma, defining competency-based learning objectives, building modular learning pathways, improving practical learning systems, integrating stronger pedagogical approaches, developing an Activity Bank, redesigning the Educator Guide, and publishing the full ecosystem across website, PDF, GitHub, and multilingual formats. The final result should remain philosophically grounded and community-driven while becoming more focused, more teachable, more scalable, and more educationally effective.

# SWOT Analysis

# Strengths

## Identity

The diploma has a strong and recognizable identity. It presents a clear educational worldview and maintains consistency across its chapters and themes. This gives the program cohesion and memorability.

## Messaging

The messaging is direct and understandable. Complex ideas are generally communicated in accessible language and connected to real-world issues that adult learners can relate to.

## Conviction

The diploma communicates its ideas with confidence. It avoids overly neutral or corporate educational language and instead presents Bitcoin as a meaningful subject worthy of serious study.

## Practical Steps

The program includes practical pathways into Bitcoin usage and participation. Learners are not limited to theory and are introduced to actionable concepts and tools.

## Global & Grassroots Format

The diploma is adaptable to community-led and grassroots environments. It does not depend heavily on expensive infrastructure or institutional systems to function.

## Social Learning

The diploma supports discussion, participation, and collaborative learning. It is designed to be experienced socially rather than only consumed individually.

## Dialogue

Dialogue is built into the learning process. Learners are encouraged to reflect, discuss, and compare perspectives throughout the program.

## Conversations

The diploma creates ongoing conversations around money, technology, and society. This helps learners connect the material to their own experiences and environments.

## Themes

Thematic consistency across the diploma helps reinforce the broader educational narrative and keeps the material coherent.

## Order

The diploma generally follows a logical progression from foundational concepts toward more advanced ideas. This supports learner orientation and conceptual development.

# Weaknesses

## Length

The overall duration of the diploma is currently one of its most significant weaknesses. High attrition is extremely harmful to the long-term success and effectiveness of the program.

According to the current Educator Guide structure, each module is designed around approximately 90 minutes. It is also unclear whether this timing assumes the use of activities or excludes them. Across ten chapters, this creates a total estimated learning time somewhere between 10 and 20 hours depending on implementation style, pacing, and educator interpretation.

There is currently too much variation in duration and delivery expectations between cohorts and educators. The diploma needs to become shorter, clearer, and more efficient without reducing educational quality or learner outcomes.

## Vague Scope

The diploma attempts to cover many domains simultaneously, including economics, history, technology, philosophy, and practical Bitcoin usage. The overall boundaries of the program are not always clearly defined.

## Blurred Lines with Multi-Disciplinary Scope

The program sometimes moves between educational categories without clearly distinguishing between technical explanations, philosophical interpretation, and broader social commentary.

## Weak Learning Objectives

Learning objectives are not always precise or measurable. Some sections lack clear statements describing what learners should specifically know or be able to do after completion.

## Lack of Competency Mapping

There is limited mapping between lessons, skills, competencies, and learner outcomes. This makes it difficult to track progression consistently.

## Lack of Measurable Outcomes

The diploma currently lacks strong measurable outcome systems. Learner progress and mastery are difficult to evaluate systematically.

## Lack of Structured Review

Review and consolidation systems are limited. Important concepts are revisited through repetition, but not always through structured reinforcement.

## Not Enough Educator Dependency

The diploma does not yet provide enough educator support structures, guidance systems, implementation standards, or teaching scaffolding to ensure consistent delivery quality across environments.

## Broad Scope

The range of subjects covered is extensive. This increases cognitive load and creates challenges for pacing and prioritization.

## Synchronous vs Asynchronous Mode Unclear

The diploma does not clearly define whether it is primarily designed for synchronous teaching environments, asynchronous learning, or hybrid delivery.

## Lack of Localisation / Personalization

The material is globally oriented but not always adaptable enough to local economic, cultural, or educational contexts.

## Repetition

Several ideas and themes reappear frequently across chapters. While reinforcement can be useful, some repetition affects pacing efficiency.

## Timing

Session timing and workload expectations are not always clearly aligned with realistic teaching conditions.

## Technical Details Are Not Always Prerequisites for Beginners

Some technical sections introduce concepts that are not necessary prerequisites for understanding or using Bitcoin as a beginner. As a result, understanding these details becomes an unnecessary learning objective for both students and educators, increasing cognitive load without proportionate educational value.

## Educator Guide Does Not Provide Enough Value

The Educator Guide currently does not provide sufficient added value for teachers. In some areas it mirrors the Student Book rather than complementing it with implementation guidance, facilitation support, teaching strategies, pacing support, or classroom management assistance.

## Practical Content Is Not Structured Through PPP

The diploma includes practical content, but practical learning is not consistently structured using the PPP method: Presentation, Practice, and Production. This limits the effectiveness of skill acquisition and applied learner confidence.
