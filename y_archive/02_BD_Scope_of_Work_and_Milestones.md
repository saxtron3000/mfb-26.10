# 1. Scope of Work

## 1.1 Purpose

Redesign the Bitcoin Diploma so that it remains philosophically grounded and community-driven while becoming:

- shorter;
- clearer;
- more modular;
- more practical;
- more captivating;
- more teachable;
- more scalable;
- more educationally effective.

The redesign must not replace the identity or foundations of the diploma. It must maintain relevance and increase educational impact as Bitcoin education matures.

---

# 2. Core Workstreams

## 2.1 Curriculum Restructuring

### Purpose
Reduce attrition and cognitive overload while preserving quality.

### Work Required
- Review full diploma structure.
- Identify essential content.
- Identify optional content.
- Identify advanced content.
- Identify repeated content that can be consolidated.
- Identify technical content that is not a beginner prerequisite.
- Clarify chapter/module sequence.
- Clarify what each section is for.
- Clarify relationship between economics, history, technology, philosophy, and practical Bitcoin usage.
- Define what belongs in the core diploma and what belongs elsewhere.

### Outputs
- Revised curriculum map.
- Essential / optional / advanced content map.
- Reduced-scope learner pathway.
- Updated sequencing proposal.
- List of content to cut, move, merge, or mark optional.

---

## 2.2 Competency-Based Learning Objectives

### Purpose
Make the diploma measurable and teachable.

### Work Required
- Define precise learning objectives.
- Convert vague objectives into measurable outcomes.
- Map objectives to competencies.
- Map competencies to activities.
- Map competencies to assessment evidence.
- Distinguish knowledge, skills, and attitudes.
- Define what a learner should know, understand, discuss, analyze, and do.

### Outputs
- Learning objective framework.
- Competency map.
- Objective-to-activity alignment map.
- Objective-to-assessment alignment map.
- Draft graduate profile.

---

## 2.3 Modular Learning Pathways

### Purpose
Support different learner needs without weakening curriculum integrity.

### Work Required
- Explore whether the diploma remains one modular course or becomes layered.
- Consider a practical fast path through the same material.
- Consider Layer 1 and Layer 2 structure:
  - Layer 1: essentials and practical use.
  - Layer 2: advanced theoretical and technical content.
- Decide whether advanced content such as Bitcoin for Organizations belongs inside a second layer.
- Clearly mark required vs optional vs advanced content.

### Outputs
- Modular pathway proposal.
- Practical fast path proposal.
- Layered pathway proposal, if approved.
- Decision log for content placement.

### Not Yet Decided
- Whether the final structure is one diploma with optional content or a layered diploma.
- Whether Layer 1 / Layer 2 becomes the official architecture.

---

## 2.4 Practical Learning Systems

### Purpose
Move practical learning from exposure to competence.

### Work Required
- Identify all practical Bitcoin skills currently included.
- Identify missing practical skills.
- Decide which practical skills are essential for beginners.
- Structure practical content using PPP:
  - Presentation.
  - Practice.
  - Production.
- Address self-custody barriers realistically.
- Clarify Lightning vs on-chain practical teaching.
- Build realistic learner scenarios.

### Outputs
- Practical skills map.
- PPP structure for practical activities.
- Real-world use case activities.
- Self-custody teaching notes.
- Lightning vs on-chain teaching guidance.

## 2.5 Activity Bank

### Purpose
Strengthen engagement, participation, review, and applied learning.

### Work Required
- Build an activity bank aligned to diploma objectives.
- Integrate activities directly into the diploma flow.
- Include individual, pair, group, and whole-class formats.
- Include online-compatible versions of activities.
- Include review and consolidation activities.
- Include practical production tasks where learners demonstrate competence.
- Include activity metadata:
  - objective;
  - duration;
  - materials;
  - grouping;
  - delivery mode;
  - educator notes;
  - expected evidence of learning;
  - common misconceptions.

### Outputs
- Activity Bank.
- Activity-to-objective map.
- Online adaptation notes.
- In-person adaptation notes.
- Review activity set.

- Avoid assessing the wrong thing.
- Consider decentralized teaching environments.
- Design rubrics and checkpoint mechanisms.

### Outputs

- Certification principles.
- Rubrics.
- Evidence standards.
- Decentralized verification questions.
- Certification decision log.

# 4. Definition of Completion

The redesign is complete when the diploma is:

- shorter, clearer, more practical, and more captivating;
- aligned to competency-based learning objectives;
- supported by a redesigned Educator Guide;
- supported by an Activity Bank;
- structured for review and consolidation;
- published as Student Book and Educator Guide PDFs;
- published on the website;
- available as raw Markdown files in GitHub;
- prepared for AI translation and multilingual publishing;
- clear enough for educators to implement;
- practical enough for learners to build real competence;
- serious enough to remain the standard for Bitcoin education.
