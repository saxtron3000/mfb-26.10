# Slide 1 — Why Redesign the Diploma?

## On Slide

### Bitcoin Diploma Redesign
#### Maintaining Relevance. Increasing Impact.

* Leader in Bitcoin education
* Strong identity and global reach
* Time for structural evolution

### Keywords

* Retention
* Modularity
* Scalability
* Outcomes
* Educator support

### Suggested Visual

A simple “Current State → Future State” transformation diagram.

Left side:
* long
* rigid
* inconsistent pacing
* educator friction

Right side:
* modular
* focused
* scalable
* engaging

### Speaker Notes

* Explain that this is not a replacement of the diploma
* The goal is evolution, not reinvention
* Emphasize maintaining identity while improving delivery and outcomes
* Mention that community feedback and educator experience drove this process

# Slide 2 — Key Findings (SWOT Summary)

## On Slide

### Strengths

* Identity
* Practical learning
* Grassroots format
* Social learning
* Conviction

### Weaknesses

* Attrition
* Length
* Weak structure
* Technical overload
* Educator friction

### Opportunities

* Modular learning
* Better pedagogy
* Activity bank
* Global scalability

### Suggested Visual

2x2 SWOT matrix with only keywords.

Use icons instead of text blocks:
* globe
* puzzle pieces
* educator icon
* clock
* pathway arrows

### Speaker Notes

* Focus heavily on attrition and duration
* Mention unclear pacing and inconsistent implementation
* Explain that technical depth is sometimes beyond beginner requirements
* Position modularity and educator empowerment as the biggest opportunities

# Slide 3 — Core Direction of the Redesign

## On Slide

### From:
* Linear
* Long
* Dense
* One-size-fits-all

### To:
* Modular
* Practical
* Flexible
* Outcome-driven

### Core Priorities

* Clear learning objectives
* Reduced cognitive load
* Better pacing
* Practical PPP structure
* Stronger educator support

### Suggested Visual

A modular pathway diagram:

Core modules in center line.
Optional modules branching outward.

Show:
* Core pathway
* Optional advanced modules
* Activity integration

### Speaker Notes

* Explain the idea of flexible pathways
* Teachers choose pathways suited to learners
* Optional modules allow specialization
* Practical learning becomes more intentional and structured
