---

# Executive Summary

The Bitcoin Diploma redesign seeks to improve:

- completion rates;
- educational impact;
- practical competence;
- educator usability;
- consistency of delivery;
- scalability;
- long-term maintainability.

The redesign is not intended to replace the identity, philosophy, or purpose of the Diploma.

---

# Applied Scope Assumptions

- Target duration: maximum 8 hours.
- Working architecture: 4 modules of approximately 2 hours.
- In-person first.
- Single Diploma with optional advanced sections.
- Core / Nice-to-Have / Advanced categorization.
- Certification design deferred until after learning outcomes and assessment systems are established.
- Knowledge, Competency, and Outcome-based objectives.
- Practical learning target increased from approximately 20% toward approximately 40%.
- Practical skills should follow PPP (Presentation → Practice → Production).
- Advanced technical content should move into advanced pathways.
- Educator Guide becomes a primary teaching support system.
- Activity Bank becomes both embedded and standalone.
- Localization preparation is included.
- Localization implementation is excluded.
- AI may assist adaptation in the future but may not alter learning objectives.

# Definition of Completion

The redesign is complete when:

- Diploma can be delivered within approximately 8 hours.
- Learning outcomes are clearly defined.
- Competencies are mapped to modules, activities, and evidence.
- Practical learning is significantly increased.
- Essential and optional content are clearly separated.
- Advanced content is appropriately separated.
- Timing assumptions are documented.
- Delivery assumptions are documented.
- Assessment framework is completed.
- Measurable outcomes are defined.
- Educator Guide is redesigned.
- Activity Bank is operational.
- Review systems are integrated.
- Student Book is published.
- Educator Guide is published.
- Website version is published.
- GitHub source files are published.
- Translation-ready source files are published.
- Localization preparation is completed.
