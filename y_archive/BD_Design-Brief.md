# BD_Design-Brief

# Pre-Overhaul Questions

## Why These Questions?

A diploma redesign is not incremental revision—it is a reset of foundational assumptions about purpose, audience, structure, and outcomes. Before diving into content changes, we need to agree on the answers to these questions. Some decisions unlock others. Some depend on earlier choices. This document walks through those dependencies systematically.


---

## Foundation Questions

These drive everything else. Answer these first.

### 1. Purpose

**What is the diploma for?**

* What problem does it solve?
* Why this format instead of a workshop, guide, or short course?
* What should learners be able to do, understand, and care about by the end?

### 2. Audience

**Who is it for?**

* Who is the primary learner?
* What prior knowledge is assumed?
* Is it for beginners, self-learners, teachers, or mixed groups?
* What age, literacy, and learning context is assumed?

### 3. Delivery Model

**What is the default delivery mode?**

* In-person first?
* Online first?
* Self-study with support?
* One core version or two separate versions?

*Depends on: Q1 (Purpose), Q2 (Audience)*


---

## Structure & Pacing

How the diploma is organized and timed.

### 4. Structure

**How is it organized?**

* Linear, modular, or semi-modular?
* What must come first?
* What can stand alone?
* Can teachers reorder or shorten safely?

*Depends on: Q1 (Purpose), Q7 (Outcomes)*

### 5. Time Model

**What is the intended pacing?**

* Are lessons 60, 90, or 120 minutes?
* Is there one default duration?
* Is there a short and extended version?
* What is the total time commitment (hours/weeks)?

*Depends on: Q2 (Audience), Q3 (Delivery), Q4 (Structure)*


---

## Learning & Teaching

How students learn and how teachers facilitate.

### 6. Learning Model

**How are students expected to learn?**

* Discussion, activity, reading, lecture, or mixed?
* What is the role of teacher explanation versus student discovery?
* What kind of classroom culture is assumed?

*Depends on: Q1 (Purpose), Q2 (Audience)*

### 9. Teacher Role

**What does the course assume about the teacher?**

* Facilitator, instructor, coach, or discussion leader?
* How much subject knowledge is needed?
* What support must be built into the guide?
* What does a teacher need to succeed with this diploma?

*Depends on: Q6 (Learning Model), Q3 (Delivery)*


---

## Quality & Consistency

How learning is measured and what remains fixed.

### 7. Outcomes

**What should students leave with?**

* What are the whole-program outcomes?
* What are the module outcomes?
* Which outcomes are essential (non-negotiable)?
* What interim checkpoints should teachers use to track progress? (By Module 3? Module 7?)

*Informs: Q8 (Assessment), Q10 (Adaptation Boundaries)*

### 8. Assessment

**How will learning be checked?**

* None, continuous, final, or mixed?
* Is there a practical component?
* What counts as passing?
* What does the diploma actually certify?
* How does assessment align with the outcomes in Q7?

*Depends on: Q7 (Outcomes)*

### 10. Adaptation Boundaries

**What can be changed locally, and what cannot?**

* What is fixed?
* What can be adapted?
* How much flexibility is acceptable before consistency breaks?

**Examples:**

* **Fixed:** Learning Outcomes are the same for all graduates, regardless of context.
* **Adaptable:** Examples, local references, timing, classroom structures, instructional methods.
* **Flexible with guardrails:** Module sequence (can be reordered by experienced teachers if Learning Outcomes remain the same).

*Depends on: Q7 (Outcomes), Q4 (Structure)*


---

## Strategic Reflection

Why redesign now, and how will we know it worked?

### 11. Why Change the Diploma?

**What is not working now?**

* From teacher feedback: ___
* From student outcomes data: ___
* From completion/dropout data: ___
* From internal observation: ___
* Are the problems about content, structure, pacing, pedagogy, or assessment?
* Which issues are repeated across contexts?
* Which issues are just internal preferences?

### 12. Desired Outcomes of the Redesign

**What are we trying to achieve?**

* Easier to teach?
* Easier to adapt to local contexts?
* Better learner understanding?
* Higher completion rates?
* More consistency across regions?
* Something else?

### 13. Success Metrics

**How will we know the redesign worked?**

For each desired outcome in Q12, define:

* What does success look like?
* How will we measure it? (e.g., baseline vs. target completion rate)
* Who will collect the data?
* When will we review?


---

## Other Important Questions

### 14. Scope

**What is essential, what is overload, and what should be moved?**

* What is essential to the diploma?
* What is overload (too much content, too much time)?
* What should be cut?
* What should be merged?
* What should be moved to optional extensions or separate training?

*Depends on: Q1 (Purpose), Q5 (Time Model)*

### 15. Coherence Across the Program

**How do the modules connect?**

* Do the 10 modules build conceptually on each other, or are they standalone?
* Is there spiral review (concepts revisited and deepened)?
* What is the learning arc from Module 1 to Module 10?
* How does this coherence affect reordering (Q4) and adaptation (Q10)?

*Informs: Q4 (Structure), Q7 (Outcomes)*

### 16. Technology Assumptions

**What technology is required or assumed?**

* Do students need internet access?
* Can lessons work offline?
* What devices are assumed (laptops, phones, paper-based)?
* Does the diploma assume Nostr platform access, or is it platform-agnostic?
* How does technology vary across global contexts?

*Depends on: Q2 (Audience), Q3 (Delivery)*

## Non-Negotiables

**Before redesign begins, make explicit: What will never change?**

## Decision Flow: Where to Start


1. **Answer Foundation Questions first** (Q1, Q2, Q3) — these unlock everything else
2. **Then answer Learning & Teaching** (Q6, Q9) — these shape how content is delivered
3. **Then answer Quality & Consistency** (Q7, Q8, Q10) — these define what passes and what can flex
4. **Then answer Structure & Pacing** (Q4, Q5) — these operationalize the earlier decisions
5. **Then answer Strategic Reflection** (Q11, Q12, Q13) — these ensure the redesign is evidence-based and measurable
6. **Finally, address Scope** (Q14, Q15, Q16) — now you know what to cut, keep, and move
