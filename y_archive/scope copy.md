# Executive Summary

This document defines the scope, milestones, and deliverables for the Bitcoin Diploma redesign.

The redesign is intended to improve:

- Completion rates
- Educational impact
- Practical competence
- Educator usability
- Consistency of delivery
- Long-term scalability

The redesign is not intended to replace the identity, philosophy, or purpose of the Diploma.

---

# Applied Design Decisions

The following decisions have now been incorporated into scope planning:

- Target duration: maximum 8 hours
- Working architecture: 4 modules of approximately 2 hours
- In-person first
- Single Diploma with optional advanced sections
- Core / Nice-to-Have / Advanced categorization
- Certification deferred until learning outcomes are finalized
- Knowledge + Competency + Outcome-based objectives
- Practical learning target increased from approximately 20% to approximately 40%
- PPP required for practical skills
- Advanced technical content moved into advanced sections
- Educator Guide becomes a primary teaching support system
- Activity Bank becomes both embedded and standalone
- Localization considered during design but implemented later
- AI may adapt content but not learning objectives

# Scope of Work

## 1. Curriculum Architecture

### Purpose

Reduce duration, reduce cognitive load, and improve retention.

### Deliverables

- Four-module architecture proposal
- Core content map
- Nice-to-have content map
- Advanced content map
- Content reduction recommendations
- Revised sequencing

### Success Criteria

- Maximum target duration of 8 hours
- Clear separation between essential and advanced learning
- Reduced duplication

---

## 2. Learning Outcomes Framework

### Purpose

Create measurable learning expectations.

### Deliverables

For every module:

#### Knowledge Objectives

What learners should know.

#### Competency Objectives

What learners should be able to do.

#### Outcome Objectives

What changes should occur in capability or confidence.

### Success Criteria

- Every activity linked to objectives
- Every objective linked to evidence of learning

---

## 3. Practical Learning Redesign

### Purpose

Increase active participation and practical competence.

### Deliverables

- Practical skills map
- PPP implementation framework
- Real-world scenarios
- Practical exercises
- Guided application activities

### Success Criteria

- Practical learning increases toward approximately 40%
- Every practical skill follows PPP

---

## 4. Activity Bank

### Purpose

Improve participation and engagement.

### Deliverables

#### Embedded Version

Integrated into Educator Guide.

#### Standalone Version

Independent resource hosted separately.

### Success Criteria

- Activities mapped to objectives
- Activities available for multiple delivery contexts

---

## 5. Educator Guide Redesign

### Purpose

Transform the Educator Guide into an essential implementation tool.

### Deliverables

- Facilitation guidance
- Timing guidance
- Discussion prompts
- Activity procedures
- Practical lesson procedures
- Review procedures
- Assessment suggestions
- Classroom management guidance
- Online adaptations

### Success Criteria

- Guide complements rather than duplicates Student Book
- Guide becomes essential for delivery

---

## 6. Review & Consolidation System

### Purpose

Strengthen retention and transfer of learning.

### Deliverables

For every module:

- Knowledge review
- Reflection
- Skills check
- Competency check

### Success Criteria

- Review becomes intentional rather than accidental

---

## 7. Advanced Learning Pathway

### Purpose

Separate advanced learning from beginner learning.

### Deliverables

- Advanced content inventory
- Optional advanced modules
- Technical content recommendations

### Success Criteria

- Advanced content no longer overloads beginners

---

## 8. Assessment Foundation

### Purpose

Prepare for future certification design.

### Deliverables

- Competency map
- Evidence map
- Assessment framework draft

### Note

Certification design is intentionally deferred.

Learning outcomes will drive certification design.

---

## 9. Localization Preparation

### Purpose

Prepare for future adaptation without fragmenting the curriculum.

### Deliverables

- Localization guidelines
- Standardized elements
- Adaptable elements
- AI guardrails

### Success Criteria

- Structure remains standardized
- Learning objectives remain standardized

---

## 10. Publishing System

### Deliverables

- Student Book PDF
- Educator Guide PDF
- Website publication
- GitHub Markdown source files
- Translation-ready source files

# Definition of Completion

The redesign is complete when:

- Diploma can be delivered within approximately 8 hours
- Learning outcomes are clearly defined
- Practical learning is significantly increased
- Advanced content is separated appropriately
- Educator Guide is fully redesigned
- Activity Bank is operational
- Review systems are integrated
- Student Book and Educator Guide are published
- Website and GitHub versions are published
- Curriculum is ready for future localization
