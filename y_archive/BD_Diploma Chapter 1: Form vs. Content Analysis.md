# Diploma Chapter 1: Form vs. Content Analysis

**Purpose:** Separate presentation/design issues from conceptual/pedagogical issues


---

## FORM Issues (Presentation, Design, Layout, Styling)

These are about *how* content is presented, not *what* is being taught.

* **Missing visual assets** — Illustrations, diagrams, infographics needed
* **Definition boxes too flat** — Redesign as dictionary-style entries (phonetics, part of speech structure)
* **Table styling** — Tables look cold/generic (MS Word) — need design refresh
* **Spacing between headers excessive** (p.23) — Layout adjustment needed
* **Capitalization inconsistency** — Standardize title case (The Psychology of Money vs Definition of scarcity vs There are two types of scarcity)
* **Color inconsistency** — Purple & formatting vary throughout; page 3 is black not purple
* **Formatting inconsistency** (p.20–21) — Bold/normal/white/orange applied inconsistently
* **"DONATE" button too blunt** — Soften the messaging/wording
* **Page breaks unnecessary** (p.13–17) — Every question gets its own page; consolidate
* **Printed vs. digital** — Digital needs to be fillable/interactive; printed needs quality focus
* **Store of Value table design** — Layout/readability issue
* **Bitcoin column instruction awkward** (p.28) — "Don't fill in" is clunky; just delete the column
* **Aristotle quote placement** — Move after questions, before Discussion (ordering/flow)

**Total: 13 form issues**


---

## CONTENT Issues (Concepts, Pedagogy, Learning Objectives, Clarity)

These are about *what* students are learning and how well it's taught.

* **Introduction not tied to chapter** — 1.0 doesn't connect sufficiently to the actual content
* **Lead questions unclear** — Necessity and pedagogical purpose unclear
  * Why these four? Are they necessary scaffolding?
* **Section 1.1 text unclear & only references first question** — Text about "determine the five most essential reasons" only connects to first lead question, not the rest → Delete or rewrite
* **Discussion: Why Do We Need Money? scope mismatch** — Title references first question only, but content requires discussion of all four questions → Reframe title or reorganize
* **"What is Money?" discussion belongs in 1.2** — Currently in 1.1 but should be in Definition section (1.2) → Content reorganization
* **Summary uses "trade" without prior introduction** — Word appears without being defined earlier in section
* **"Money serves several functions" is weak** — Generic intro; needs more concrete, direct framing
* **Class Exercise: "What function of money is this an example of?" is pedagogically poor** — Bad activity design (not just layout, but the learning objective and approach)
* **Store of Value table conceptually confusing** — "Price" and "key player" are vague; students won't understand what they represent
* **Digital fiat money concept missing from 1.5** — Current binary (physical/digital) misses the nuance that digital fiat is both
* **Definition of scarcity isn't actually a definition** — Conceptual clarity issue
* **Sequencing in 1.5 unclear** — Should flow Cryptocurrency → Stablecoins → CBDCs; current order muddled
* **Lead questions pedagogy unclear** — Are these think-pair-share prompts? If so, format needs modeling (think → pair → share → reflect cycle not shown)

**Total: 13 content issues**


---

## Issues That Are Both (Form + Content)

These affect both presentation and the underlying learning:

* **Page count explosion (169 → 236)** — May indicate verbosity (form) or conceptual bloat (content)
* **Class Exercise design** — Pedagogically weak (content) AND poor layout/interactivity (form)
