# Watch: Why Every Bitcoiner Should Run Their Own Node
## Keywords
Bitcoin node, decentralization, security
## Activity Type
Video viewing
## Participation

## Duration
5 minutes
## Description
Students watch the first five minutes of a video explaining what Bitcoin nodes are and why running one may matter. The educator stops before the setup instructions and emphasizes that nodes are neither miners nor wallets: they independently run the Bitcoin protocol and maintain an updated blockchain copy.
## Objectives

- Learners distinguish the roles of a Bitcoin node, a miner, and a wallet.

## Materials
Internet access and video playback.
## Preparation

## Procedure
### Setup

### Step 1
Open the video.
### Step 2
Watch only the first five minutes, then stop when the speaker begins explaining setup.
### Step 3
Emphasize that nodes are not miners or wallets: they run the Bitcoin protocol and maintain an updated copy of the blockchain.
## Review
### Key Points
Nodes help ensure the network is decentralized and secure.
