# What Is Money?
## Keywords
money, value, fiat money
## Activity Type
Class discussion
## Participation

## Duration

## Description
Students compare trading a piece of candy for a US$1 bill and for a $1 Monopoly bill.
## Objectives

## Materials
Monopoly bills or counterfeit bills of the country’s currency; one piece of candy per student.
## Preparation
Place a piece of candy on each student’s desk.
## Procedure
### Setup
Ask students not to eat the candy yet.
### Step 1
Ask who would trade their candy for a US$1 bill, then who would trade it for a $1 Monopoly bill.
### Step 2
Ask why or why not.
### Step 3
Discuss what makes one bill desirable and another worthless, what gives money value, where money comes from, who decides how much to print, and why more money is not printed and distributed equally.
## Review
### Key Points

### Discussion questions
What gives money its value?
