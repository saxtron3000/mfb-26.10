# Barter Game

## Keywords

**Barter** **Commodity Money** **Double Coincidence of Wants**

## Activity type

Game

## Participation

## Duration

5 minutes for the barter round

## Objective

- Participants experience and compare direct barter with exchange using a commodity as money.

## Description

Participants trade paper cards showing goods they have and goods they want, attempting to find matching exchange partners before time expires. They repeat the activity with macaroni as a common [medium of exchange](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/lexicon/en/#medium-of-exchange), then compare the ease of trade and the limitations of using a commodity as money.

#### Preparation

**Materials**

- Paper sheets cut into rectangles
- Macaroni or a similar item

Prepare cards showing each student’s “have” and “want.”

#### Setup

Participants keep their original “want” and trade their “have” until the two match.

#### Procedure

##### Step 1

Allow five minutes for trading. Participants return to their seats when their new “have” matches their original “want,” or when time expires.

##### Step 2

Ask how many participants achieved their goal after one, two, or three trades. Discuss the benefits and drawbacks of barter.

##### Step 3

Give each student one piece of macaroni and assume every good costs one piece of macaroni. Repeat the goal and discuss commodity money.

#### Key points

#### Discussion questions

Why could some participants trade and others not? Was the macaroni round easier? What are the drawbacks of macaroni as money?
