# Final Class Discussion: How Did Your Perspective Change?
## Keywords
**Money** **Bitcoin** **Reflection** **Learning**

## Activity Type
Discussion

## Participation

## Duration

## Objective

- Participants reflect on how their understanding of money has changed and identify a next step for continued learning.

# Description
Participants revisit the questions they answered in the first Chapter 1 activity, compare their original ideas with their current views, and discuss what changed. The final reflection asks participants to identify a next step and consider how they can use their new knowledge about money and Bitcoin to empower themselves.
# Preparation

## Materials
- Participants’ responses from the first Chapter 1 activity.

## Setup
Ask participants to answer again: Why do we need money? What is money? Who controls money? What gives money value?
# Procedure
## Step 1
Return to the first Chapter 1 activity and compare new answers with old answers.
## Step 2
Compare and discuss the original answers and questions. Ask whether something changed.
## Step 3
Ask: What is my next step, and how can I use this new knowledge to empower myself?
# Review
## Key Points

## Discussion questions
How did your perspective change? What is your next step?
