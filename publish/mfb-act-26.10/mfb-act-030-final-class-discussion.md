# Final Class Discussion: How Did Your Perspective Change?
## Keywords
money, reflection, Bitcoin Diploma
## Activity Type
Final class discussion
## Participation

## Duration

## Description
Students revisit their initial answers about money, compare them with their current views, and identify a next step.
## Objectives

## Materials
Students’ responses from the first Chapter 1 activity.
## Preparation

## Procedure
### Setup
Ask students to answer again: Why do we need money? What is money? Who controls money? What gives money value?
### Step 1
Return to the first Chapter 1 activity and compare new answers with old answers.
### Step 2
Compare and discuss the original answers and questions. Ask whether something changed.
### Step 3
Ask: What is my next step, and how can I use this new knowledge to empower myself?
## Review
### Key Points

### Discussion questions
How did your perspective change? What is your next step?
