# Is Bitcoin Sound Money?
## Keywords
Bitcoin, sound money, money characteristics
## Activity Type
Class discussion
## Participation

## Duration

## Description
Students revisit a money comparison table and compare Bitcoin with other forms of money.
## Objectives

## Materials
Money comparison table.
## Preparation

## Procedure
### Setup
Return to the money comparison table from Chapter 2.
### Step 1
Review the characteristics: durable, portable, uniform, acceptable, scarce, and divisible.
### Step 2
Compare cows, cigarettes, diamonds, euros, and Bitcoin against each characteristic.
### Step 3
Complete the totals in the comparison table.
## Review
### Key Points

### Discussion questions
How does Bitcoin compare with other forms of money?
