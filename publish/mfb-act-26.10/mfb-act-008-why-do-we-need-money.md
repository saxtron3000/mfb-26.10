# Why Do We Need Money?
## Keywords
money, value, class discussion
## Activity Type
Class discussion
## Participation

## Duration

## Description
Students answer five questions about money, share their ideas in groups, and identify common ideas across the class.
## Objectives

## Materials

## Preparation

## Procedure
### Setup
Ask students to consider practical uses for money, including necessities and desired items.
### Step 1
Students answer: Why do we need money? What is money? Who controls money? What gives money value? What question do you have about money?
### Step 2
In groups, students share answers, vote on five favorite answers, and share five favorite questions.
### Step 3
Revisit the answers and questions at the end of the Bitcoin Diploma.
