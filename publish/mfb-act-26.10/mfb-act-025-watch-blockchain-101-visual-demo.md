# Watch: Blockchain 101 – A Visual Demo
## Keywords
blockchain, blocks, Bitcoin
## Activity Type
Video viewing
## Participation

## Duration
Up to 17 minutes
## Description
Students watch most or all of “Blockchain 101 – A Visual Demo” as a visual alternative or complement to generating a SHA-256 hash. The video illustrates how blocks are linked, allowing learners to observe how changes to data affect a chain and to connect the demonstration to the course discussion of blockchain verification.
## Objectives

- Learners observe how linking blocks makes changes to earlier blockchain data visible.

## Materials
Internet access and video playback.
