# Watch: Blockchain 101 – A Visual Demo

## Keywords

**Blockchain** **Blocks** **Hashes**

## Activity type

Video discussion

## Participation

## Duration

Up to 17 minutes

## Objective

- Participants observe how linking blocks makes changes to earlier blockchain data visible.

## Description

Participants watch most or all of [“Blockchain 101 – A Visual Demo”](https://youtu.be/_160oMzblY8) as a visual alternative or complement to generating a SHA-256 hash. The video illustrates how blocks are linked, allowing participants to observe how changes to data affect a chain and to connect the demonstration to the course discussion of blockchain verification.
