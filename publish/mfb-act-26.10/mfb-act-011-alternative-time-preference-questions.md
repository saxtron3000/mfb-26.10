# Alternative Time Preference Questions for Discussion
## Keywords
time preference, money, savings, consumption
## Activity Type
Class discussion
## Participation

## Duration

## Description
Students use guided questions to connect time preference with money, entrepreneurship, consumption, saving, and interest rates. The discussion treats money as a way people communicate value in a free market and considers how understanding present and future preferences can inform personal decisions.
## Objectives

- Learners connect time preference with saving, consumption, and interest rates through discussion.

## Materials

## Preparation

## Procedure
### Setup

### Step 1
Ask why time preference is included in a chapter about money.
### Step 2
Ask how time preference helps entrepreneurs decide whether to save or spend, and how understanding it can help people live better lives.
### Step 3
Discuss money as a communicator of value, the choice between immediate consumption and future savings, and how time preference appears through interest rates.
## Review
### Key Points
Money communicates value in a free market. Time preference influences consumption and savings decisions.
### Discussion questions
How can learning about time preference help you make better money decisions?
