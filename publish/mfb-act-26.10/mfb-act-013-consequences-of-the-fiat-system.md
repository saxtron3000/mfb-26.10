# Consequences of the Fiat System
## Keywords
fiat system, society, history
## Activity Type
Group Socratic discussion
## Participation

## Duration

## Description
Students take part in a Socratic discussion about possible consequences of the fiat system for individuals, society, and their country. They connect broad ideas to local history and personal examples, listening to differing perspectives and considering how monetary systems shape everyday life.
## Objectives

- Learners consider possible consequences of the fiat system through local, historical, or personal examples.

## Preparation

## Procedure
### Setup

### Step 1
Ask students to identify consequences individuals and society experience because of the fiat system.
### Step 2
Ask about consequences in their country, its history, and how people were affected.
### Step 3
Invite personal examples in an interactive session.
## Review
### Key Points

### Discussion questions
What consequences has the fiat system had in your country?
