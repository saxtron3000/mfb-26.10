## Description
Students watch a non-technical Bitcoin overview designed for complete beginners. The viewing gives the class a shared starting point for identifying the problem Bitcoin addresses, its basic features, and questions that require further discussion or verification.
## Objectives

- Learners build a beginner-friendly understanding of Bitcoin and share the video’s main ideas in their own words.
