# Verification
## Keywords
don’t trust, verify, mining, nodes, proof of work
## Activity Type
Class demonstration
## Participation

## Duration
10 minutes
## Description
Students use dice to illustrate the work required to find a winning block and the ease of verification by nodes.
## Objectives

## Materials
At least six dice or an online dice-rolling tool.
## Preparation
Choose a starting target for rolls, such as three or more dice showing 1.
## Procedure
### Setup
Explain that buyers, sellers, and node runners each verify at different stages, and that miners try many times to find a block below the difficulty adjustment.
### Step 1
Students or a designated miner roll six dice repeatedly. The goal is for at least three dice to show 1; each roll represents a mining hash calculation.
### Step 2
Adjust the target to four 1s if it is too easy or two 1s if it is too difficult.
### Step 3
When the target is hit, the miner broadcasts the result and the class verifies the number of 1s. Repeat several times.
## Review
### Key Points
Finding a winning set may take many rolls, while verification takes seconds.
### Discussion questions
Why is a winning block fast for nodes to verify?
