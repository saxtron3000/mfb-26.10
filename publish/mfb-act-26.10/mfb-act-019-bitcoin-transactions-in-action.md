# Bitcoin Transactions in Action
## Keywords
Bitcoin transaction, sender, receiver, node, miner, blockchain
## Activity Type
Role-play class activity
## Participation
Participants act as senders, receivers, nodes, or miners.
## Duration

## Description
Participants role-play senders, receivers, nodes, and miners to create, verify, validate, and add peer-to-peer Bitcoin transactions to a classroom blockchain. Transaction notes, coins, approval tables, and dice make the flow of a payment visible, including checks for valid addresses, sufficient funds, and double spending.
## Objectives
- Participants practice the roles of senders, receivers, nodes, and miners in validating and adding a simulated Bitcoin transaction.
## Materials
Transaction notes; coins; approval tables; dice.
## Preparation
Assign participants the roles of sender, receiver, node, or miner.
## Procedure
### Setup
Explain each role: senders create and broadcast; receivers receive and verify; nodes validate; miners add transactions to the blockchain.
### Step 1
Senders write the amount and receiver on a transaction note, sign it, and pass the note and coins to the receiver.
### Step 2
Receivers check the note and coin count, then approve or reject. Nodes validate addresses, sufficient funds, and absence of double spending.
### Step 3
Miners review approved transactions, roll dice, and the miner with the smallest number adds the transaction to the blockchain. Participants keep track of their coin balances.
## Review
### Key Points
Once added to the blockchain, a transaction cannot be changed or reversed.
