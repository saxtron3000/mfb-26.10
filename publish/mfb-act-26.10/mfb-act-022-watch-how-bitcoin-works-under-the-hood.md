## Description
Students watch “How Bitcoin Works under the Hood” to reinforce the technical ideas introduced in the Bitcoin Diploma. The educator can pause or follow the viewing with questions that help learners connect the video’s explanations to Bitcoin’s transactions, blocks, nodes, mining, and consensus.
## Objectives

- Learners deepen their understanding of a technical Bitcoin concept presented in the video.
