# Bitcoin Diploma 2026.10 cohort assessment grid

> **Print setting:** A4 landscape, 100% scale. This document has one competency-reference page and one cohort-progress page. If your renderer does not honour the page break, insert one before Page 2.

## Page 1 — Competency reference

The Bitcoin Diploma aims for every learner to attain and demonstrate its required competencies. Use one row per learner on Page 2 to record that evidence. Record one final result for each assessed `C` competency: `MS` = Meets standard; `NY` = Not yet; blank = not assessed. `P` may be used as an informal **practising** marker while teaching. Decimal `C` identifiers are required competency criteria and observable look-fors, not separate results.

### Assessed competencies, criteria, and educator notes

- **`C1` Money and comparison** — `C1.1` why money is used; `C1.2` barter and beginner money history; `C1.3` functions and properties; `C1.4` compare two forms of money.
- **`C2` Modern-money trade-off** — `C2.1` introductory inflation, purchasing power, banking, or CBDC trade-offs; `C2.2` a guided local, observed, or realistic example.
- **`C3` Digital double spending** — `C3.1` the digital-copy problem and shared verification.
- **`C4` Bitcoin rules and money properties** — `C4.1` public rules and key properties; `C4.2` roles, proof of work, supply, halving, and sats; `C4.3` a balanced sound-money judgement.
- **`C5` Guided digital-asset comparison** — `C5.1` comparison using control, supply, verification, custody, or counterparty; `C5.2` distinguish learning or use from trading, promotion, or speculation.
- **`C6` Wallet information and custody** — `C6.1` public and secret information; `C6.2` custody choices; `C6.3` hot and hardware wallets.
- **`C7` Safe response to risk** — `C7.1` scams, errors, and high-risk requests; `C7.2` when to stop, verify, or seek help.
- **`C8` Payment and access pathways** — `C8.1` on-chain and Lightning; `C8.2` acquisition or exchange pathways and risks.
- **`C9` Supported first access and receipt** — `C9.1` wallet navigation and receive information; `C9.2` a receive-or-send flow and checks; `C9.3` first access and receipt of bitcoin, or a safe simulation.
- **`C10` Recovery readiness** — `C10.1` protect and explain recovery information using prepared or simulated materials.
- **`C11` Connect: local Bitcoin-as-money task** — `C11.1` safe task sequence and verification; `C11.2` connect the task to its local purpose, realistic alternative, trade-offs, safety, and next step.

`C11` is the educator-curated **Connect** stage of the Diploma's Problem → Solution → Action → Connect learning arc.

### Educator notes

- Deliver the required knowledge and guided practice during the cohort, then mark `MS` only when every listed competency criterion has sufficient, safe evidence—not attendance, activity completion, or confidence. Mark `NY`, give focused practice, and reassess when evidence is incomplete, inaccurate, unsafe, or overly prompted.
- Theory evidence must be observable learner action: explain, compare, justify, or apply. Do not record a competency from an assertion or self-description alone.
- `C9`, `C10`, and `C11` require direct educator observation. A safety-critical error is `NY`.
- Theory and practice are separate requirements: theory is `C1`–`C8`; practice is `C9`, `C10`, and `C11`.
- Never record seed phrases, private keys, credentials, balances, or unnecessary personal information. `C12` and `C13` are formative competencies only.
- Use the [Educator Guide's operational assessment protocol](mfb-bd-eg-26.10.md#operational-assessment-protocol) for task IDs, detailed prompting, accommodations, evidence requirements, safety-critical errors, reassessment, and calibration.
