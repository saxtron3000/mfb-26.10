# Bitcoin Diploma Beginner Lexicon

This lexicon lists Bitcoin and Bitcoin-adjacent terms that a beginner may need explained. Line references are to the official [Student Guide](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/). **First** is the earliest explicit appearance; **Later** lists subsequent explicit appearances. `Not present` means the term is not stated explicitly in the Student Guide.
