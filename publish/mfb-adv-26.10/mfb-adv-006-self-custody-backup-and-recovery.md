# Advanced Module 6: Self-Custody, Backup, and Recovery

## TL;DR

Study how people can hold bitcoin directly while preparing for loss, failure, and succession. Self-custody can increase control, but it also creates serious responsibilities that require a deliberate recovery plan.

## Purpose

This module examines custody models, signing devices, seed phrases, backups, passphrases, multisignature arrangements, recovery testing, and inheritance planning. The emphasis is on understanding trade-offs and creating safe procedures, not on using a particular product.

## Learners will be able to

- compare custodial, single-signature, and multisignature custody models;
- identify recovery, loss, theft, and coercion risks in a fictional scenario;
- outline a backup and recovery plan without revealing secrets; and
- explain why a plan should be tested in a safe environment before it is needed.

## Suggested learning sequence

1. Map custody responsibilities and possible points of failure.
2. Compare backup and signing models using fictional cases.
3. Design a recovery procedure for a simulated wallet.
4. Review inheritance and handover questions with appropriate local expertise.

## Boundary

> **Callout – Never collect or inspect learner secrets**
>
> Never collect, inspect, photograph, transmit, or store learner seed phrases or private keys. This educational module is not personal security, legal, estate-planning, or financial advice.
