# Advanced Module 4: Cryptography

## TL;DR

Explore the cryptographic tools that let Bitcoin verify ownership and protect the integrity of data. Learners work from demonstrations to concepts: hashes, keys, signatures, and verification.

## Purpose

Cryptography is a foundation for understanding Bitcoin without trusting it as magic. This module introduces one-way functions, hash functions, public-key cryptography, digital signatures, and their roles in Bitcoin transactions and blocks.

## Learners will be able to

- explain the difference between encryption, hashing, and digital signatures;
- demonstrate how a small change in input creates a different hash;
- describe the roles of private keys, public keys, and signatures in authorization; and
- explain why secure systems rely on both mathematics and careful human practice.

## Suggested learning sequence

1. Compare secret messages, hashes, and signatures.
2. Generate and compare hashes from small input changes.
3. Model signing and verification without exposing real keys.
4. Connect cryptographic verification to transactions and block integrity.

## Boundary

> **Callout – Use safe examples only**
>
> Activities must use safe examples, simulations, or test environments. Never ask learners to share seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or personal account details.
