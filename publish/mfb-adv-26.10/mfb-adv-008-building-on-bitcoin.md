# Advanced Module 8: Building on Bitcoin

## TL;DR

Explore how people build payment, application, and community infrastructure on Bitcoin, beginning with the Lightning Network. Learners examine how design choices affect settlement, liquidity, usability, privacy, resilience, and responsibility.

## Purpose

Bitcoin’s base layer makes deliberate trade-offs. Builders and users may use additional layers, protocols, and services to support different applications. This module uses Lightning as a central case study for channels, routing, liquidity, payment failures, and operational trade-offs.

## Learners will be able to

- explain why systems may build on Bitcoin rather than change its base rules;
- distinguish on-chain settlement from Lightning payment flows;
- describe channels, routing, liquidity, and common payment trade-offs at a conceptual level; and
- evaluate an application idea in terms of users, trust assumptions, safety, and local relevance.

## Suggested learning sequence

1. Identify base-layer trade-offs and reasons to build additional systems.
2. Model a Lightning payment path, including a failed route.
3. Compare usability, liquidity, privacy, and operational responsibilities.
4. Prototype or critique a locally relevant Bitcoin application using a simulation.

## Boundary

> **Callout – Analyse; do not require real-world adoption**
>
> Do not require learners to use personal funds, open channels, run infrastructure, or adopt a named service. Building on Bitcoin involves real technical and operational trade-offs; this module teaches analysis, not product endorsement.
