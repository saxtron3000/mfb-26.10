Reference counts are direct Markdown links to registered lexicon URLs in the current Student Guide, counted on 1 October 2026. Guide terms link to the exact URL registered for their `BD-LEX-###` ID in [the terms register](mfb-lx-26.10-terms.md). “Lexicon entries” counts distinct word-or-phrase entries rather than occurrence rows or individual aliases. “Term entries found” counts entries with at least one verified Student Guide definition link.

This lexicon lists Bitcoin and Bitcoin-adjacent terms that a beginner may need explained. It is the terminology authority for the Student Guide. Each entry has a stable anchor for links from that guide and other published learning materials.

Link a term only when it is used in a Bitcoin, economic, or computing sense and the linked definition explains that specific use. Leave ordinary English uses and incidental word matches unlinked; use the more specific entry when one exists, such as Lightning liquidity for channel capacity.

For **privacy**, link only uses where the word is a noun in its own right (for example, `protect privacy` or `financial privacy`). Do not link attributive uses that modify another noun, including shared modifiers in lists (for example, `privacy risks` or `privacy and safety boundaries`).

Student Guide line links are a snapshot of the current [Student Guide](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/programs/bitcoin-diploma/en/student-guide/). Each referenced line contains the listed term or an appropriate alias linked to its registered definition URL. Its line number opens the containing section, so a term with multiple matching lines appears more than once. Longer listed phrases take precedence over terms within them. Supporting terms used only in the Educator Guide are marked `Not present`.

Guide traceability was refreshed on 8 October 2026 from the published Student Guide. Run `python3 tools/refresh_lexicon.py --check` after guide edits to audit the snapshot and linked-use metadata.
