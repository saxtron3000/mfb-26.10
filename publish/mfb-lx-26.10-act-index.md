Reference counts are direct Markdown links to registered lexicon URLs in the current activities, counted on 1 October 2026. Activity terms link to the exact URL registered for their `BD-LEX-###` ID in [the terms register](mfb-lx-26.10-terms.md). “Linked entries” counts distinct entries with at least one verified activity definition link.

This lexicon lists Bitcoin and Bitcoin-adjacent terms used in activities. It is the terminology authority for the activities. Each listed term has a stable anchor for links from the activities.

Link a term only when it is used in a Bitcoin, economic, or computing sense and the linked definition explains that specific use. Leave ordinary English uses and incidental word matches unlinked; use the more specific entry when one exists, such as Lightning liquidity for channel capacity.

For **privacy**, link only uses where the word is a noun in its own right (for example, `protect privacy` or `financial privacy`). Do not link attributive uses that modify another noun, including shared modifiers in lists (for example, `privacy risks` or `privacy and safety boundaries`).

Activity line links are a snapshot of the current activity files. Each listed line contains the listed term or an appropriate alias linked to its registered definition URL. Longer listed phrases take precedence over terms within them.
