If I’m a teacher responsible for an MFB node, what should I expect students to know or be able to do by the end of the course?

Understand is a must for all of the points below:

Must:

1. Understand the history of money (evolution)  
   1. Activities \- Barter  
   2. Comparing different forms of money and how the characteristics differ  
2. Understand the problems with our current monetary system  
   1. Fractional Reserve Banking  
   2. Connect with Nodes, Runy said he had a great AI activity that he created  
3. Understand the difference between Custodial and non-custodial bitcoin holdings  
   1. Put it into practice. Educators should be comfortable using Bitcoin Testnet coins to help students “touch” self-custodied Bitcoin.  
4. Understand what a hardware wallet is and why it’s beneficial  
   1. There are very low cost hardware wallet options available, educators must know how to access them. (seedsigner, Krux, Jade)  
5. Understand the different ways to buy and sell Bitcoin (impartiality is crucial)  
   1. My First Bitcoin could be the main resource for helping people navigate this part of their Bitcoin Journey. We could have onboarding guides for certain exchanges, wallets, and other tools that help people onramp and offramp Bitcoin (telegram p2p bot, robosats, hodlhodl, etc)  
6. Understand the basic functions of a node and miner in the Bitcoin Network  
7. Lightning vs onchain  
8. Know how to transact onchain and lightning

Nice to have:

1. Understand exactly the history of the bretton woods agreement, geopolitical agreements, etc.  
2. Understand exactly how a node works  
3. Understand exactly how a miner works  
4. Understand what a hash is and all the parts of the Bitcoin network in which it is involved  
5. Understand how a lightning node works and why channel management is necessary   
6. Understand how to obtain Bitcoin in a Non KYC manner, and why it’s beneficial  
7. Understand what a hardware wallet is and the levels of hardware wallet security (open source, Bitcoin only, airgapped)
