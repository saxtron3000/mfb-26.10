# Bitcoin Diploma Educator Guide Outline

## Purpose

This document defines the structure of the redesigned Educator Guide.

The Educator Guide should function as a facilitation engine, not as a duplicate of the Student Book.

---

# Educator Guide Principles

The guide should help educators:

- prepare quickly;
- pace each block realistically;
- choose activities;
- manage discussion;
- correct misconceptions;
- adapt examples locally;
- select Custom / Bitcoin as Money modules;
- protect learner safety and autonomy.

### General Teaching Tips

- Educators should feel allowed to say "not yet" when a student question would pull the class into a complex side topic. This does not shut down curiosity. It protects the structure, timing, and learning objectives of the activity. A useful response is: "That is an important question, but it belongs later. We will come back to it when the timing is right." This helps avoid rabbit-holing students before they have the foundation to understand the answer.

## 7. Legacy Content Migration Guidance

- Use the legacy table-of-contents migration notes in `06_plan.md` to decide whether old content belongs in Core, Custom, or Advanced material.
- Do not treat old `OUT` markings as deletion when the same item is marked Custom or Advanced.
- Keep Block 3 technical material practical: use it only to support safer self-custody, verification, and transaction responsibility.
- Split topics carefully when needed: beginner concepts may stay in Core while deeper analysis moves to Advanced material.
- Place daily-life Lightning wallet setup, local spending, and merchant scenarios in Custom / Bitcoin as Money unless the local delivery plan requires them earlier.
- Use Custom modules for local money examples, local inequality or debt discussions, and real tools used by the community.

# Custom Block Selection Checklist

Educators should choose Custom / Bitcoin as Money modules based on:

- student needs;
- local Bitcoin tools;
- local risks;
- local opportunities;
- available devices and connectivity;
- realistic next steps;
- educator confidence and competence.
