# Bitcoin Diploma 2026.10 Workspace Index

## Purpose

This index explains how the working documents in `workspace/` guide the creation of the Bitcoin Diploma 2026.10 curriculum.

The repository now separates three kinds of material:

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [`workspace/`](./) | Working documents that guide content creation, including architecture, competencies and observable criteria, design rules, activities, assessment, terminology, and publication planning. |
| [`publish/`](../publish/) | Official curriculum documents and supporting publication materials. |
| [`review/`](../review/) | Documents used to review quality, consistency, annotations, and release readiness across the project. |

These areas have different roles. Working documents support planning and maintenance. Publication documents are the official learner- and educator-facing curriculum. Review documents identify issues and actions but do not replace the published curriculum.

For a repository-wide introduction, see the [root README](../README.md).

## Current Working Direction

The redesign uses the four-stage **Problem → Solution → Action → Connect** learning arc:

1. **Problem** — Understanding Money
2. **Solution** — How Bitcoin Works
3. **Action** — Using Bitcoin Safely
4. **Connect** — Bitcoin in Your Community

All four modules together are the Diploma. Modules 1 and 2 provide the shared core; educators can adapt practical examples, tools, resources, and delivery in Modules 3 and 4 within the required competency and safety boundaries. Module 4 is the educator-curated **Connect** stage: educators use their local subject-matter expertise to link the Problem, Solution, and Action learning to a real local need or opportunity, a realistic alternative, and a responsible next step. It does not require a learner to adopt Bitcoin or treat a local tool as a recommendation.

The working assumptions are:

- a minimum eight-hour beginner pathway that includes teaching, practice, assessment, feedback, and recording, with no upper limit when learners need more time;
- educator-facilitated delivery in person or online;
- a complete low-tech, offline-capable print/PDF route;
- no app or connectivity requirement for learning, assessment, completion, or certification;
- shared learning in Modules 1–3, with educator-curated, locally relevant Module 4 practice required for Diploma completion;
- practical learning in every module;
- separate learner, educator, activity, assessment, and optional microlearning components;
- English-first drafting followed by simplicity, tone, terminology, and translation-readiness review;
- two required, non-compensatory assessment components: educator-evaluated theory and educator-observed practical competency;
- optional app self-assessment that never supplies certification evidence;
- Advanced design after the beginner Diploma is stable and published; and
- Bitcoin-only, open-source, community-led education without financial, legal, tax, accounting, compliance, or investment advice.

`06_plan.md` remains the active authority for architecture and content boundaries.

## Content Ownership Rules

- Use `06_plan.md` for architecture and content-boundary decisions.
- Keep the seven competency statements and required look-fors in the [Educator Guide's operational assessment protocol](../publish/mfb-bd-26.10/mfb-bd-eg-26.10.md#operational-assessment-protocol). Mirror them in `05_competencies.md` for planning and mapping.
- Keep learner-facing publication content in `publish/mfb-bd-26.10/mfb-bd-sg-26.10.md` and align it with `07_contents.md`.
- Use `10_activities.md` for activity boundaries, quality requirements, and release priorities. Keep detailed activities and assessment-task specifications on the [Resources Hub](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/resources/), not in the Student Guide or curriculum plan.
- Keep educator-facing publication content in the [Educator Guide](../publish/mfb-bd-26.10/mfb-bd-eg-26.10.md); use `12_educator-plan.md` to plan and check its structure.
- Keep operational assessment rules, competency wording, runnable theory interactions, and practical tasks in the [Educator Guide's operational assessment protocol](../publish/mfb-bd-26.10/mfb-bd-eg-26.10.md#operational-assessment-protocol); planning support in `13_assessment.md`; cohort records in `mfb-bd-assessment-grid-26.10.md`; and certification eligibility and governance questions in `15_certification.md`.
- Keep optional app planning in `14_microlearning-plan.md` and publication data in `publish/mfb-ml-26.10/mfb-ml-26.10.md`.
- Keep the complete Advanced inventory and Organizations source trail in `workspace/17_advanced.md`.
- Keep cross-project findings, decisions, quality checks, and release actions in `review/`.
- Draft in English first, then review simplicity, tone, terminology, accessibility, and translation readiness before publication.
- Keep printed QR codes sparse and tied to stable, high-value destinations.
- Date and cite current-data examples or replace them with evergreen explanations before publication.
- Do not turn named wallets, exchanges, tools, maps, services, or local resources into global recommendations.
