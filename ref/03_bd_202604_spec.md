# Bitcoin Diploma

## Program Title

Bitcoin Diploma

## One-Sentence Purpose

Provide a comprehensive, structured foundation in Bitcoin, money, financial systems, and digital sovereignty through a deep 10-module curriculum, available in multiple delivery formats.

## Program Summary

The Bitcoin Diploma is My First Bitcoin's flagship educational program. It offers a comprehensive journey from fundamental concepts of money and financial systems through Bitcoin's origins, technology, philosophy, and real-world applications. The program is designed for learners who want to move beyond introductory concepts and develop a nuanced understanding of money, decentralization, and Bitcoin's role in the global economy. Available in multiple delivery formats — in-person, online cohort, self-paced video, and PDF — the Diploma has been implemented by thousands of students and educators across 40+ languages worldwide.

## Why This Program Exists

The Bitcoin Diploma addresses a critical need: **deep, accurate Bitcoin education** grounded in economic history, philosophy, and practical understanding—not speculation or ideology. Since 2022, when the first Diploma was taught in El Salvador's public schools, the program has evolved into a globally recognized standard for Bitcoin education. It exists to:

* Empower individuals with the knowledge to understand and critically evaluate Bitcoin
* Equip educators and community leaders with a complete curriculum
* Build financial literacy around money, systems, and sovereignty
* Create a shared language and framework for Bitcoin discussion globally

The program reflects My First Bitcoin's core principle: **teach how to think, not what to think**.

## Who It Is For

### Primary Audience

* Learners seeking in-depth understanding of Bitcoin and money (high school through adult)
* Educators and trainers delivering Bitcoin education in schools and communities
* Students in formal education settings
* Self-directed learners committed to comprehensive study
* Community members participating in My First Bitcoin nodes and networks

### Secondary Audience

* Journalists and communicators covering Bitcoin

### Prior Knowledge or Requirements

* **Preferred:** Completion of Introduction to Bitcoin (but not required)
* **Required:** Basic reading/video comprehension ability
* **Not required:** Technical background, financial training, or prior Bitcoin knowledge
* **Commitment:** 8–10 weeks, 5–8 hours per week (varies by delivery format; see Delivery Format section)

### Who It Is Not For

* Traders seeking market analysis or price prediction (explicitly out of scope)
* Those looking for investment advice or quick financial gains
* Beginners wanting a 30-minute overview (Introduction to Bitcoin is better suited)
* Developers seeking deep cryptographic or blockchain technical specs (specialized resources exist)

## Core Learning Goals

By completing the Bitcoin Diploma, learners should be able to:

* **Explain what money is** functionally and historically, and describe how monetary systems work
* **Analyze problems with fiat money systems**, including inflation, debt, and centralization
* **Understand Bitcoin's creation, design, and philosophy**, including the problems it attempts to solve
* **Describe how Bitcoin works** at a conceptual level (mining, transactions, security, the blockchain)
* **Safely acquire, store, and use Bitcoin**, including self-custody and responsible practices
* **Evaluate claims about Bitcoin** critically using economic and technical reasoning
* **Envision Bitcoin's potential role** in future financial systems and personal sovereignty

The focus is on **understanding, critical thinking, and long-term perspective**—not on short-term trading or speculation.

## Key Topics or Content Areas

### Module 1: What Is Money?

Understanding money functionally and psychologically

* Why money matters and what it does
* Definition of money
* Functions of money (medium of exchange, store of value, unit of account)
* Properties of sound money (divisibility, portability, durability, acceptability, scarcity, etc.)
* Types of money (commodity, representative, fiat)
* Psychology of money (how people think about and value money)

### Module 2: The History of Money

Evolution from barter to digital systems

* From barter systems to commodity money
* Metal-based money and coinage
* Representative money and credit systems
* Modern fiat currency emergence
* Introduction to digital currency concepts

### Module 3: What Is Fiat Money?

Deep dive into current monetary systems

* History of fiat money systems
* How the fiat system works (central banking, monetary policy, fractional reserves)
* Role of central banks and government
* Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) — concepts and implications
* Sustainability and failure modes of fiat systems

### Module 4: How Problems Lead to Solutions

Economic challenges and the search for alternatives

* Inflation: causes, effects, and historical examples
* Global debt and financial inequality
* Limitations of current financial systems
* Historical search for decentralized alternatives
* Why Bitcoin emerged when it did

### Module 5: What Is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin at conceptual and technical level

* Satoshi Nakamoto and Bitcoin's creation story
* Bitcoin's purpose and core innovation (decentralized consensus)
* How Bitcoin works: keys, addresses, and transactions
* Bitcoin as "sound money" — comparing to commodity money properties
* Core differences from fiat money

### Module 6: How to Use Bitcoin

Practical skills for acquisition, storage, and transactions

* Acquiring Bitcoin responsibly
* Understanding and choosing wallets
* Mobile wallets and best practices
* Receiving and sending transactions
* Security principles: custody, seed phrases, backup
* "Don't Trust, Verify" — self-validation basics

### Module 7: Using Bitcoin in Daily Life

Real-world applications and advanced use cases

* The Lightning Network and payment channels
* Types of Lightning wallets
* Setting up and using Lightning wallets
* Sending and receiving Lightning transactions
* Everyday use: buying coffee and groceries with Bitcoin
* Merchant and business perspectives

### Module 8: How Bitcoin Works

Technical foundations without requiring computer science background

* Cryptography basics (public/private keys, signing, verification)
* Security through cryptography
* The UTXO (Unspent Transaction Output) model
* How transactions are structured
* Immutability and chain integrity

### Module 9: How Does Bitcoin Mining Work?

Consensus mechanism and network security

* Bitcoin nodes and their roles
* Miners and mining pools
* The mempool and transaction propagation
* How transactions are confirmed
* Proof-of-work and network security
* Mining economics and incentives

### Module 10: What Future Can Bitcoin Build?

Philosophy, vision, and possibilities

* Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) versus Bitcoin
* Philosophy of Bitcoin: decentralization, neutrality, transparency
* Benefits and potential of Bitcoin adoption
* Bitcoin's role in an empowered future
* Vision for financial sovereignty

### Back Matter

Supporting resources for learners

* Real-world case studies (El Salvador, communities using Bitcoin)
* Additional resources and recommended reading
* Key concepts and terminology guide
* Glossary of Bitcoin and financial terms

## Delivery Format

### Mode

Multiple delivery options to suit different learning styles and contexts:


1. **In-Person Classroom** — Live instruction with an educator, typically 1-2 hours per week
2. **Online Live Cohort** — Virtual classes with an instructor and peer group, typically 1-2 hours per week
3. **Self-Paced Video Course** — Watch videos anytime, with optional forums and community; progress at your own pace
4. **PDF/Book Format** — Complete text available for reading or distribution
5. **Hybrid** — Combination of video and live sessions

### Structure

* **10 modules** of 90 minutes of core content each
* Each module includes:
  * Learning objectives
  * Structured content (presentations, discussions, activities)
  * Key concepts summary
  * Discussion prompts or reflection questions
  * Optional extension activities
* **Self-paced video course specifics:**
  * Primary video lesson per module (20–40 minutes)
  * Optional supplementary videos (5–15 min each)
  * Optional downloadable reading/PDF
  * Optional discussion forums (asynchronous)
  * Optional worksheets and activities
  * Lifetime access to enrolled learners

### Duration

* **In-Person/Online Live Cohort:** 5–10 weeks, 1-2 hours per week = \~8-10 total hours
* **Self-Paced Video:** 10 sessions at recommended pace (1–2 modules per week), or flexible self-determined schedule (as fast, or as slow as learners wish)
* **Self-Study with PDF/Book:** 6-8 hours weeks depending on depth of engagement
* **Accelerated Format:** Some educators compress into intensive workshops (1-2 days)

## What Participants Actually Do

* **Watch video lessons** (self-paced option) or **attend live classes** (cohort option)
* **Read or review content** from the Bitcoin Diploma book/PDF

## Completion or Success Criteria

### Completion Markers

**In-person/cohort:**

* Engagement in class discussions and activities
* Submission of module reflections or assignments

**Self-paced video:**

* Viewing all 10 module video segments
* (Optional) Completion of reflection questions

**Self-study book:**

* Reading all modules and back matter

### Success is Measured By

Learner ability to:

* Articulate how money works and its role in society
* Explain Bitcoin's purpose and core innovations
* Describe how to safely acquire and store Bitcoin
* Critically evaluate claims about Bitcoin
* Understand and discuss concepts like scarcity, decentralization, and sovereignty
* Feel confident asking informed follow-up questions

### Certification

**Completion Certificate:** Issued to learners who complete their chosen format (in-person, cohort, or self-paced video). The certificate recognizes achievement in Bitcoin financial literacy education and can be shared with employers, schools, or communities.

**No formal credentials.** The Diploma is educational in nature and does not confer formal academic credentials, but it is recognized globally as a rigorous Bitcoin education program.

## Scope & Limitations

### Explicitly in Scope

* Understanding money, financial systems, and Bitcoin
* Technical foundations of how Bitcoin works
* Practical skills: acquiring, storing, using Bitcoin
* Bitcoin's role in economics and society
* Critical evaluation of Bitcoin claims
* Real-world adoption case studies

### Explicitly NOT in Scope

* Cryptographic algorithm details (beyond conceptual level)
* Mining hardware specifications or profitability calculations
* Trading strategies or market analysis
* Investment advice or financial recommendations
* Endorsement of specific wallets, exchanges, or services
* Regulation of Bitcoin (varies by jurisdiction)
* Advanced smart contracts or layer-2 protocols (introduced but not deep-dived)

### Prepares for Later Learning

The Diploma provides a foundation for:

* Advanced technical study (Bitcoin Core, development)
* Economics research
* Specialized roles (educator, entrepreneur, developer)
* Informed participation in Bitcoin and cryptocurrency policy discussions

## Disclaimer

This curriculum is **educational in nature**.

It is intended to support understanding, discussion, and critical thinking.

It should **NOT** be interpreted as:

* Financial advice
* Investment guidance
* Legal counsel
* Tax information
* Endorsement of any product or service

Educators should contextualize learning to their specific students and local regulations.
