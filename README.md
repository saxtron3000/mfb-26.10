# Bitcoin Diploma 2026.10

This repository contains the working guidance, publication sources, and review materials for My First Bitcoin's October 2026 Bitcoin Diploma curriculum.

The redesign preserves the Diploma's open-source, community-led, Bitcoin-only identity while making the beginner pathway shorter, clearer, more practical, easier to teach, and easier to adapt across communities.

## Curriculum at a Glance

The beginner Diploma uses four modules:

1. Understanding Money
2. How Bitcoin Works
3. Using Bitcoin Safely
4. Bitcoin in Your Community

The current design uses an approximately eight-hour, educator-facilitated pathway with a complete low-tech and offline-capable option. Module 4 connects the shared learning to locally relevant Bitcoin use under approved safety and assessment guardrails.

Assessment has two required, non-compensatory components: educator-evaluated theoretical understanding and educator-observed practical competency. The optional app supports learner-owned recap and self-assessment only; it is not required and does not provide certification evidence.

## Review Workflow

1. Check [`review/00_todo.md`](review/00_todo.md) before beginning release work.
2. Use the applicable source of truth in `workspace/` to resolve design or policy questions.
3. Apply approved decisions to the relevant files in `publish/`.
4. Record new cross-project findings in the appropriate `review/` document.
5. Check links, terminology, objective alignment, safety, accessibility, translation readiness, and current-data citations before publication.

Review documents identify problems and proposed actions. They do not override the working source-of-truth documents by themselves.

## Contribution Guardrails

- Keep the curriculum educational, Bitcoin-only, open-source, and community-led.
- Do not present financial, legal, tax, accounting, compliance, or investment advice.
- Do not turn named wallets, exchanges, tools, maps, services, or local resources into global recommendations.
- Keep required learning and assessment usable without an app or continuous connectivity.
- Use “module” for the four required Diploma sections and keep Module 4 broader than spending alone.
- Draft in clear English first and review for simplicity, terminology, accessibility, and translation readiness.
- Date and cite current-data claims or replace them with evergreen explanations.

## Public Message

The Bitcoin Diploma is being reorganized, not replaced. The 2026.10 curriculum is designed to be shorter, clearer, more practical, and more locally adaptable while preserving its mission.
