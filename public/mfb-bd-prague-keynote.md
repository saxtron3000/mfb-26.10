# MFB BD Prague Keynote Handoff

## Use

Use `mfb-bd-prague.md` for the fuller stakeholder brief.

# Public Thesis

The Bitcoin Diploma is becoming shorter, clearer, more practical, and more locally adaptable while preserving its open-source, community-led, Bitcoin-only identity.

# Speaker Notes

Keep the tone public and reassuring.

Say:

- The Diploma is being reorganized, not replaced.
- It is becoming shorter, but not shallower.
- Legacy content is moving into the right places: Core, Custom, or Advanced.
- The fourth block gives educators local choice.
- Practical workshops and demonstrations are becoming more central.

Avoid:

- listing all Advanced module titles;
- sounding like the old Diploma failed;
- promising final certification details;
- overexplaining technical Bitcoin topics;
- using internal planning terms when a plain contrast works better.

---

# Before / After

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Long and dense pathway | 8-hour 4-block pathway |
| Beginner and advanced content mixed together | Core, Custom, and Advanced separation |
| Activities embedded unevenly | Dedicated Activity Bank |
| Educator support uneven | Educator Guide as facilitation engine |
| Local adaptation unclear | Educator-selected Custom block |

---

# Call To Action

For educators: help test Custom modules.

For communities: share local tools, use cases, constraints, and practical needs.

For contributors: help build activities, visuals, and educator support tools.

For supporters: help make publication, translation, educator readiness, and the October 2026 release possible.
