# Bitcoin Diploma 2026.10 — Elevator Pitch

## Core proposition

The Bitcoin Diploma is being reorganized, not replaced. The 2026.10 edition preserves My First Bitcoin’s open-source, community-led, Bitcoin-only mission while making the beginner pathway clearer, more practical, safer to deliver, and easier to adapt locally.

## What stays the same

- Free, open-source education that communities can read, translate, adapt, and improve.
- A Bitcoin-only focus, without trading, token promotion, or financial advice.
- Independent reasoning, learner autonomy, and respectful discussion of evidence and trade-offs.
- Community-led delivery with local relevance and shared global competency standards.
- Serious beginner education that puts safety and understanding before action.

## Why update the Bitcoin Diploma

The existing Bitcoin Diploma has valuable foundations. The update gives those foundations a clearer route for beginners and educators:

- less unnecessary technical overload and stronger sequencing
- more discussion, practice, reflection, and learner explanation
- clearer educator support for pacing, safety, adaptation, and assessment
- a practical local application without treating one community’s tools or circumstances as universal; and
- clearer separation between required beginner learning and future Advanced study.

## The learning pathway

The Bitcoin Diploma is a four-module pathway that takes at least eight hours. Eight hours is the shortest planned route, not a maximum; educators take more time whenever learners need it. The advertised route includes teaching, practice, assessment, feedback, and recording.

| Role | Module | Learner focus |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | Understanding Money | Explain the money problems Bitcoin responds to. |
| Solution | How Bitcoin Works | Explain why Bitcoin was created and its key properties. |
| Action | Using Bitcoin Safely | Practise beginner custody and safety skills under supported conditions. |
| Connect | Bitcoin in Your Community | Complete an educator-curated, locally relevant Bitcoin-as-money task. |

The arc is **Problem → Solution → Action → Connect**. All four modules are required. Module 4 is educator-curated because educators understand their communities’ needs, risks, tools, and opportunities; it still follows the shared competency and safety standards. A well-reasoned conclusion that a path is not suitable locally is valid learning.

## What makes it stronger

- Practical learning in every module, using a clear explain → practise → apply → reflect rhythm.
- A Student Guide, Educator Guide, assessment grid, and activities on the [Resources Hub](https://programs.myfirstbitcoin.org/resources/) with distinct roles.
- Low-tech, offline-capable delivery: no app, personal device, or continuous connection is required for learning, assessment, completion, or certification.
- The public My First Bitcoin App launch is postponed, so it is not part of the 2026.10 learner pathway or assessment and certification evidence.
- A two-part, non-compensatory assessment: 50% educator-evaluated theoretical evidence and 50% educator-observed practical evidence. A certificate requires all seven assessed competencies to meet the required standard in both components. The named educator assesses the learner and issues the certificate; My First Bitcoin does not centrally evaluate, issue, verify, or guarantee learner competency.
- Wallet choice taught as a decision path: open-source software is preferred where appropriate for transparency, but every option has trade-offs and no wallet is the global recommendation.
- Advanced technical and specialist topics kept outside the Bitcoin Diploma, where they can be studied with the time and context they require.

## The payoff

Learners get a coherent, confidence-building introduction to money, Bitcoin, responsibility, and local relevance. Educators get clearer facilitation and assessment tools. Communities get meaningful local adaptation without losing the shared standard that gives the Bitcoin Diploma its value.

## Public message

The Bitcoin Diploma is becoming shorter, but not shallower; more practical, but not less rigorous; and more locally adaptable, while preserving a shared global standard.

## Release direction

The public publication target is October 2026. The publication package includes the offline-capable print/PDF materials, an online PDF, and website-ready content published through Sanity. This is a framework-first release: it publishes the teaching package and assessment framework without claiming that every local route is ready for certificated delivery. The work benefits from educator review, community input, pilot and field learning, translation support, contribution to activities and visuals, and publication support.
