# Bitcoin Diploma Redesign — Elevator Pitch

## Core proposition

- The Bitcoin Diploma is becoming shorter, clearer, more practical, and easier to teach.
- The redesign improves the learning experience without changing the mission.
- It turns a strong but dense curriculum into a scalable learning pathway for beginners, educators, and communities.

## What stays the same

- Open-source access.
- Community-led delivery.
- Bitcoin-only education.
- Independent reasoning and learner autonomy.
- Local adaptability and global accessibility.
- Serious, practical education rather than promotion or hype.

## Why redesign now

- The existing content has strong value but needs a clearer learner path.
- Beginners need less cognitive overload and better sequencing.
- Educators need clearer pacing, activities, facilitation support, and adaptation guidance.
- Communities need a shared foundation that can still reflect local needs.
- Practical skills need to be taught and assessed more intentionally.

## The new learning pathway

- Approximately 8-hour completion pathway, including teaching, practice, assessment, feedback, and recording.
- Four coherent modules:
  1. **Understanding Money** — understand the monetary problem.
  2. **How Bitcoin Works** — understand Bitcoin’s design and purpose.
  3. **Using Bitcoin Safely** — learn wallets, safety, and responsible use.
  4. **Bitcoin in Your Community** — connect learning to local tools, needs, and use cases.
- Core, Custom, and Advanced content boundaries prevent beginner overload while preserving depth.

## What makes it stronger

- More discussion, practice, reflection, and real-world application.
- PPP practical learning: Presentation, Practice, Production.
- A dedicated Activity Bank for participation and local adaptation.
- An Educator Guide designed as a facilitation engine.
- Optional app self-checks, recap, and discovery resources that do not affect certification.
- A 50% educator-evaluated theoretical component and 50% educator-observed practical component. Both must meet the approved standard before certification.
- Low-connectivity and offline delivery remain part of the design.

## The strategic payoff

- A clearer pathway designed to help more learners complete the Diploma with confidence.
- Educators receive clearer tools and greater local flexibility.
- Communities get relevant learning without losing shared standards.
- Advanced study remains available without overwhelming beginners.
- Shared assessment protects the meaning and reputation of the certificate.
- The Diploma becomes easier to adopt, localize, translate, teach, and scale.

## Public message

The Bitcoin Diploma is being reorganized, not replaced. It is becoming shorter, but not shallower; more practical, but not less rigorous; more locally adaptable, while preserving a shared global standard.

## Release direction

- Public release target: October 2026.
- The next phase needs educator review, community input, contributors, translation support, pilots, and publication support.
