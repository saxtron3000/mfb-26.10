# Prague Keynote Proposal: Bitcoin Diploma 2026

## Purpose

This document proposes a 10-minute public keynote about the Bitcoin Diploma redesign.

It is separate from `public_MFB-PRAGUE-BD.md`, which remains the longer stakeholder brief.

---

# Public Thesis

The Bitcoin Diploma is becoming shorter, clearer, more practical, and more locally adaptable while preserving its open-source, community-led identity.

---

# 10-Minute Structure

## Slide 1: Bitcoin Diploma 2026

Time: 30 seconds

Key message: The Diploma is evolving, not being replaced.

Speaker note: Open by naming the redesign as a continuation of the same mission: open-source Bitcoin education led by communities.

Suggested visual: Title over a simple 4-block strip.

## Slide 2: Why Redesign?

Time: 75 seconds

Key message: The Diploma has strong foundations, but the learner path needs clearer structure.

Speaker note: Explain that the issue is not lack of content. The issue is duration, overload, pacing, and educator usability.

Suggested visual: Before / after table.

## Slide 3: What Stays The Same

Time: 60 seconds

Key message: The mission, identity, and community-led model remain.

Speaker note: Reassure stakeholders that open-source access, Bitcoin-only focus, educator empowerment, and grassroots adaptability remain non-negotiable.

Suggested visual: Four preservation anchors: Open, Community-led, Bitcoin-only, Practical.

## Slide 4: What Changes

Time: 75 seconds

Key message: The Diploma becomes shorter, clearer, more practical, and easier to teach.

Speaker note: Name the major changes: 8-hour pathway, 4 blocks, Activity Bank, stronger Educator Guide, Core / Custom / Advanced separation.

Suggested visual: Change stack or simple checklist.

## Slide 5: The 4-Block Model

Time: 120 seconds

Key message: The new structure tells a simpler learning story.

Speaker note: Walk through the four blocks:

1. Problem / Fiat
2. Solution / Bitcoin
3. Action / Self-custody
4. Custom / Bitcoin as Money

Suggested visual: Horizontal 4-block diagram.

## Slide 6: Educators Complete The Local Story

Time: 90 seconds

Key message: The fourth block is chosen by educators because they know their communities best.

Speaker note: Explain that educators choose practical Custom / Bitcoin as Money modules based on local tools, local needs, risks, and opportunities.

Suggested visual: Educator selects from Custom module bank into Block 4.

## Slide 7: A Learning Ecosystem

Time: 90 seconds

Key message: The redesigned Diploma is more than a student book.

Speaker note: Introduce Student Book, Educator Guide, Activity Bank, Custom templates, and Advanced pathways.

Suggested visual: Ecosystem diagram.

## Slide 8: What This Makes Possible

Time: 75 seconds

Key message: More learners can complete the Diploma and more educators can teach it well.

Speaker note: Describe improved completion, local adaptation, practical learning, educator usability, and advanced pathways without beginner overload.

Suggested visual: Learner pathway from Core Diploma to local use and future learning.

## Slide 9: October 2026 Call To Action

Time: 75 seconds

Key message: The public release needs educators, communities, contributors, and supporters.

Speaker note: Invite educators to test Custom modules, communities to share local use cases, contributors to build activities, and supporters to help publication and translation.

Suggested visual: Four calls to action.

---

# Before / After Table

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Long and dense pathway | 8-hour 4-block pathway |
| Beginner and advanced material mixed together | Core / Custom / Advanced separation |
| Activities embedded inconsistently | Dedicated Activity Bank |
| Educator support uneven | Educator Guide as facilitation engine |
| Local adaptation unclear | Educator-selected Custom / Bitcoin as Money block |

---

# Call To Action

For educators: help test Custom / Bitcoin as Money modules.

For communities: share local Bitcoin use cases, tools, and constraints.

For contributors: help build activities, visuals, and educator support tools.

For supporters: help make publication, translation, and educator readiness possible.
