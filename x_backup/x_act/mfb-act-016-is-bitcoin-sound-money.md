# Is Bitcoin Sound Money?
## Keywords
**Bitcoin** **Sound Money**

## Activity Type
Discussion

## Participation

## Duration

## Objective

- Participants compare Bitcoin with other forms of money using the characteristics of good money.

## Description
Participants return to the money comparison table and assess Bitcoin alongside cows, cigarettes, diamonds, and euros. Using characteristics such as [durability](../../publish/mfb-lx-26.10-sg-index.md#term-durability), [portability](../../publish/mfb-lx-26.10-sg-index.md#term-portability), uniformity, [acceptability](../../publish/mfb-lx-26.10-sg-index.md#term-acceptability), [scarcity](../../publish/mfb-lx-26.10-sg-index.md#term-scarcity), and [divisibility](../../publish/mfb-lx-26.10-sg-index.md#term-divisibility), they complete the comparison and discuss whether Bitcoin meets the characteristics of [sound money](../../publish/mfb-lx-26.10-sg-index.md#term-sound-money).
## Preparation

### Materials
- Money comparison table.

### Setup
Return to the money comparison table from Chapter 2.
## Procedure

### Step 1
Review the characteristics: durable, portable, uniform, acceptable, scarce, and divisible.
### Step 2
Compare cows, cigarettes, diamonds, euros, and Bitcoin against each characteristic.
### Step 3
Complete the totals in the comparison table.
# Review
## Key Points

### Discussion questions
How does Bitcoin compare with other forms of money?
