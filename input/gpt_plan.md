# GPT Recommendations Following the Bitcoin for Teens Discovery Session

This document captures the key strategic recommendations that emerged from the initial design discussion. They are intentionally separate from the curriculum itself and should guide future decision making throughout the project.

---

# 1. Freeze the Philosophy Before Writing Curriculum

This is the single biggest design decision.

Do **not** begin writing lessons yet.

Instead, complete the philosophical foundation first, then derive every lesson from those principles.

The recommended design sequence is:

```
Vision

↓

Educational Philosophy

↓

Design Principles

↓

Learning Framework

↓

Educator Role

↓

Student Experience

↓

Expeditions

↓

Individual Sessions
```

This order will keep the curriculum coherent and prevent individual lessons from drifting away from the project's core purpose.

---

# 2. Recruit the Teen Design Council Immediately

Do not wait until a draft curriculum exists.

The Teen Design Council should be involved while the project is still forming.

They are not reviewing finished work.

They are helping determine what the work should become.

Their role is to:

* identify blind spots
* validate language
* challenge assumptions
* contribute ideas
* ensure relevance
* keep the curriculum authentic

Design with teens.

Not simply for teens.

---

# 3. Write a Design Charter

Create a separate document that serves as the constitution of the project.

It should explain:

* Why the project exists
* What success looks like
* Which principles are non-negotiable
* How decisions are made
* How feedback is incorporated
* Who owns the final design decisions

Whenever disagreements arise, return to the Design Charter.

---

# 4. Define Agency

Agency appeared repeatedly throughout the discovery conversation.

Rather than leaving it abstract, define it through observable behaviours.

Examples might include:

* Starts projects independently
* Asks better questions
* Plans further ahead
* Uses time intentionally
* Becomes comfortable saying "I don't know"
* Researches independently
* Changes opinions when presented with evidence
* Teaches others
* Accepts responsibility
* Demonstrates initiative

These behaviours should eventually become the true learning outcomes of the curriculum.

---

# 5. Build an Inspiration Library

Do not attempt to invent everything from scratch.

Study educational approaches that have already solved similar problems.

Possible sources of inspiration include:

* Montessori
* Socratic Dialogue
* Expeditionary Learning
* Outward Bound
* Scouting
* Martial Arts pedagogy
* Apprenticeships
* Maker Spaces
* Project Based Learning

Bitcoin for Teens should borrow proven educational ideas while creating its own identity.

---

# 6. Establish a Project Vocabulary

Throughout the discussion a consistent language naturally emerged.

Preferred words:

* Gateway
* Journey
* Guide
* Expedition
* Challenge
* Quest
* Discovery
* Reflection
* Journal
* Curiosity
* Agency

Words to avoid where possible:

* Lecture
* Module
* Lesson
* Workbook
* Teacher
* Knowledge transmission

A shared vocabulary will help every future contributor write with the same voice.

---

# 7. Protect the Blank Journal

The blank journal is one of the strongest ideas within the project.

Resist the temptation to slowly transform it into a workbook.

Its emptiness is symbolic.

It communicates:

> "This is your journey."

The journal is not simply somewhere students write.

It is where they think.

It becomes:

* notebook
* sketchbook
* research log
* reflection journal
* project planner
* collection of questions
* record of personal growth

Ownership begins with a blank page.

---

# 8. Define Non-Negotiables

Create a list of principles that should never be compromised.

Examples include:

* Discovery before explanation.
* Questions before answers.
* Agency before information.
* Reflection before assessment.
* Low-tech first.
* Bitcoin is the gateway, not the destination.
* Facilitators guide rather than lecture.
* Students create more than they consume.
* Time is an ally.

Every future decision should be measured against these principles.

---

# Final Reflection

A significant shift occurred during this discovery session.

The project began as an idea for a Bitcoin course.

It evolved into something much larger:

A philosophy of adolescent education that uses Bitcoin as its gateway.

That distinction should be protected.

The long-term success of Bitcoin for Teens will not be determined by whether it teaches Bitcoin better than other courses.

Its success will come from helping young people become:

* more curious
* more thoughtful
* more self-directed
* more confident
* more reflective
* more capable of shaping their own future

Bitcoin is simply the gateway through which that transformation begins.
